Accurately predicting DNA–ligand binding affinities is critical for the scalable design of DNA aptamer-based sensors, yet the field lacks computational tools in virtual screening that balance computational efficiency with experiment-level accuracy. Here, we adapt DeltaGzip, an affinity prediction protocol combining molecular dynamics simulation with Gzip lossless compression, to DNA aptamer–ligand systems. To this end we refined the calculation of enthalpy, performed representation learning to improve the entropy calculation, and introduced a pre-screening step to filter out non-binding ligands. In the absence of suitable datasets for method validation, we have performed isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC) measurements of the binding free energy for a dopamine-binding DNA aptamer binding 22 different molecular ligands with varying affinity. Our experiments show that DeltaGzip’s predictions agree with the ITC measurements, both qualitatively and quantitatively.
