Cyclin-dependent kinase 2 (CDK2) is a key regulator of cell-cycle progression and has been implicated in cancer-related dysregulation of the p53 signaling axis. In this study, molecular docking simulations using AutoDock Vina v1.2.7 were performed to compare the binding behavior of three pharmacologically relevant compounds — Roscovitine, Nutlin-3, and Pifithrin-α — within the ATP-binding pocket of CDK2 (PDB: 1AQ1; resolution: 2.0 Å). Docking reliability was assessed by comparing results across two exhaustiveness settings (Exh = 8 and Exh = 16), with all affinity differences remaining below 0.1 kcal/mol, indicating stable convergence of the search algorithm. Among the three ligands, Pifithrin-α showed the strongest predicted binding affinity (−8.599 kcal/mol), followed by Roscovitine (−8.388 kcal/mol) and Nutlin-3 (−7.501 kcal/mol). Protein-ligand interaction profiling with PLIP revealed distinct interaction patterns for each compound. Roscovitine formed three hydrogen bonds, ten hydrophobic contacts, and one salt bridge with catalytic residues LYS33 and ASP145, consistent with its known ATP-competitive CDK2 inhibition. Pifithrin-α displayed a parallel π-stacking interaction with PHE80 and a short hydrogen bond with LEU83, suggesting a possible CDK2-binding mode that merits further experimental evaluation. Nutlin-3 showed the weakest predicted affinity and the fewest contacts, in line with its established role as an MDM2 antagonist rather than a direct CDK2 ligand. Overall, these findings provide a comparative interaction-level analysis of three p53-pathway-related compounds at CDK2 and support further structure-guided investigation of Pifithrin-α as a potential off-target binder.
