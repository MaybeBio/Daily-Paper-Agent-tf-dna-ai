### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that TQ treatment resulted in high PI3K/Akt1 pathway inhibition in p. H1047R and p. H1047L-expressing cells versus wild-type cells.  
- **Evidence pointer** Methods and Results section, location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract states that TQ treatment led to high pathway inhibition in mutant cells, but no quantitative data, dose-response curves, or statistical comparisons are provided. The term "high inhibition" is vague and unsupported.  
- **Why it matters** Without quantitative evidence, the central claim of mutation-specific TQ sensitivity cannot be verified. The magnitude of the effect, its statistical significance, and its biological relevance are all unknown.  
- **Resolution test** Provide dose-response data with IC50 values, statistical comparisons between mutant and wild-type cells, and appropriate controls. Include effect sizes and confidence intervals.
