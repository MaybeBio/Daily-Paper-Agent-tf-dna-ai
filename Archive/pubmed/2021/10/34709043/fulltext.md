Activation of hypoxia-inducible factor 2 (HIF-2) has emerged as a potent renal anemia treatment strategy. Here, the benzisothiazole derivative 26 was discovered as a novel HIF-2alpha agonist, which first demonstrated nanomolar activity (EC(50) = 490 nM, E(max) = 349.2%) in the luciferase reporter gene assay. Molecular dynamics simulations indicated that 26 could allosterically enhance HIF-2 dimerization. Furthermore, compound 26 had a good pharmacokinetic profile (the oral bioavailability in rats was 41.38%) and an in vivo safety profile (the LD(50) in mice was greater than 708 mg.kg(-1)). In the in vivo efficacy assays, the combination of 26 and the prolyl hydroxylase inhibitor, AKB-6548, was confirmed for the first time to synergistically increase the plasma erythropoietin level in mice (from 260 to 2296 pg.mL(-1)) and alleviate zebrafish anemia induced by doxorubicin. These results provide new insights for HIF-2alpha agonists and the treatment of renal anemia.
