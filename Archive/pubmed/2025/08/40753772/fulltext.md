Disruption of estrogen receptor alpha (ERalpha) by endocrine-active pesticides may contribute to lipid metabolic dysregulation. We aimed to clarify the docking dynamics of methiocarb with ERalpha and evaluate its potential to induce lipid accumulation through ERalpha activation using non-animal testing systems. Molecular docking predicted favorable binding between methiocarb and ERalpha, primarily through interactions involving the amino group. This prediction was validated using ERalpha reporter gene assays. Methiocarb-induced lipid accumulation was assessed in 3T3-L1 adipocytes, with or without co-treatment using the ERalpha antagonist methyl-piperidino-pyrazole (MPP). Methiocarb significantly activated ERalpha transcriptional activity and promoted ERalpha-dependent lipid accumulation. Co-treatment with MPP attenuated this effect, whereas antagonists for the glucocorticoid receptor (RU-486) and ERbeta (PHTPP) had no effect. Methiocarb increased the expression of adipogenic and lipogenic transcription factors, including PPARgamma, C/EBPalpha, FAS, and SREBP1, as well as the adipocyte-specific marker FABP4, in an ERalpha-dependent manner. Methiocarb binding to ERalpha promotes lipid accumulation and upregulates adipogenic/lipogenic transcriptional networks. This approach highlights the utility of ERalpha-mediated screening to identify potential metabolic disruptors among structurally related pesticides.
