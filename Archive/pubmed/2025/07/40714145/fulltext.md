Sinomenine possesses potent suppression on the nuclear factor kappa-B (NF-kappaB) pathway. The phenylpropanoid moiety was introduced into sinomenine for building dual-structure sinomenine hybrid derivatives. The software AutoDock Vina was used to dock the sinomenine hybrid derivatives with 3 key proteins (1VKX, 4KIK and 6YMN) of NF-kappaB activation, and the virtually screened derivatives were tested via molecular dynamics simulation. Three series of derivatives were synthesized by Heck cross-coupling reaction, and evaluated for their suppressive effects on NF-kappaB in HEK 293 cells transfected with plasmid pNF-kappaB-luc. Some 1-acrylate sinomenine derivatives showed more active suppressive effects than the parent sinomenine. Derivatives 1-3 of sinomenine series were more active on NF-kappaB than other three series. Derivative 3 with 1-phenethyl acrylate was the most active one.
