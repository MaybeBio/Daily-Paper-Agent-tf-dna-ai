AIM: This study aimed to assess the preventive potential of apremilast (APRE) against methotrexate (MTX)-induced hepatotoxicity through modulation of Nrf2/HO-1 and TLR4/NF-kappaB/p38 MAPK/Caspase-3 pathways. MAIN METHODS: Male rats were assigned to the control, apremilast, methotrexate, and methotrexate + apremilast groups. Histopathological investigation and analysis of the serum hepatotoxicity indicators (AST, ALT, ALP, and GGT) were used to evaluate the hepatic toxicity. Testing for hepatic MDA and GSH was conducted. The levels of hepatic TNF-alpha, IL-6, Nrf2, alpha-klotho, HO-1, and cleaved caspase-3 were measured using the ELISA method. Using an immunohistochemistry method, the expression of NF-kappaB p65 was investigated. Western blotting was used to examine the expression of TLR4 and p38 MAPK, and docking experiments were performed on all proteins that were investigated. KEY FINDINGS: The liver was significantly affected, as evidenced by higher levels of ALT, AST, ALP, and GGT as well as increased levels of hepatic MDA, TNF-alpha, IL-6, BAX, Caspase-3, and decreased GSH activity and BCL-2 expression. These outcomes were linked to inhibition of alpha-klotho/Nrf2/HO-1 signaling and activation of TLR4/NF-kappaB/p38 MAPK/Caspase-3. Co-treatment with APRE markedly improved all biochemical and pathological alterations evoked by MTX. CONCLUSION: Apremilast mitigates the harmful effects of methotrexate on the liver by activating signaling cascades that include Nrf2/HO-1, while downregulating TLR4/NF-kappaB/p38 MAPK/Caspase-3.
