Cancer is one of the most complex diseases. Its multidrug resistance and poor prognosis make the development of antitumor drugs still a difficult task. 18beta-glycyrrhetinic acid (18beta-GA) has shown great potential for tumor prevention and treatment. However, inherent defects, such as low bioavailability, limit its use. Herein, based on a molecular hybridization strategy, two series of alpha, beta-unsaturated carbonyl modified 18beta-GA derivatives were synthesized using 18beta-GA as the parent nucleus. Among them, compounds 9a, 9g and 9i exhibited the strongest antiproliferative activity, with IC(50) values ranging from 1.52 to 3.46 muM against HCT-116, SH-SY5Y and HepG2 cells. The key target of the three active derivatives was predicted to be human STAT3 through reverse target finding. Docking analysis proved that all three derivatives showed strong binding affinity to STAT3 in silico, suggesting potential as STAT3 inhibitors.
