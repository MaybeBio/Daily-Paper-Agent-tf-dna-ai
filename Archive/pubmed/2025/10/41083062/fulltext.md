ETHNOPHARMACOLOGICAL RELEVANCE: QingReXiaoZheng Decoction (QRXZD), a modern clinical empirical prescription, has demonstrated significant efficacy in treating diabetic kidney disease (DKD). However, the underlying renal protective effects of QRXZD remains uncertain. AIM OF THE STUDY: To explore the potential therapeutic mechanisms of QRXZD in DKD through network pharmacology and experimental validation. METHODS: Firstly, we employed an integrated approach combining network pharmacology and molecular docking to predict the potential targets and mechanisms of QRXZD in DKD treatment. Subsequently, a DKD rat model was established to evaluate the renoprotective effects of QRXZD. Pathological staining, biochemical assays, Western blotting, immunohistochemistry, enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay, and immunofluorescence were conducted to elucidate the underlying mechanisms of QRXZD in DKD treatment. RESULTS: Network pharmacology analysis revealed that QRXZD may exert renoprotective effects by inhibiting the PI3K/Akt signaling pathway. Molecular docking demonstrated strong binding affinity between the active ingredients of QRXZD and disease-related targets. In vivo experiments confirmed that QRXZD ameliorated renal pathological damage and delayed disease progression in DKD rats. Mechanistic studies indicated that QRXZD attenuated renal inflammation and oxidative stress while reducing tubular injury in DKD rats by suppressing PI3K/Akt/NF-kappaB pathway activation and restoring Nrf2 expression. CONCLUSION: QRXZD alleviates renal injury, inhibits renal inflammation and oxidative stress, and reduces renal tubular injury in DKD rats by regulating the PI3K/Akt/NF-kappaB pathway and restoring Nrf2 expression.
