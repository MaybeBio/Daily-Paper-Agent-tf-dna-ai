Venous thromboembolism (VTE) is a leading cause of morbidity and mortality, with limited options to prevent recurrence and vascular injury. This study identifies KDM5B as a novel VTE risk gene and therapeutic target through integrated transcriptomics and Mendelian randomization. We further demonstrate that Licoisoflavone A, a flavonoid from Glycyrrhiza glabra, binds stably to KDM5B and attenuates endothelial inflammation. In IL-1beta-stimulated endothelial cells, Licoisoflavone A downregulated KDM5B and VCAM-1, suppressed pro-inflammatory cytokines, and inhibited TNF/NF-kappaB signaling. These findings reveal a previously unrecognized anti-thrombotic mechanism of Licoisoflavone A and support its potential as a candidate therapy for VTE.
