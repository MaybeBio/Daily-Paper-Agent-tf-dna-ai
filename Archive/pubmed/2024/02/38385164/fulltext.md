Background: IkappaB kinase beta (IKKbeta) plays a pivotal role in the NF-kappaB signaling pathway and is considered a promising therapeutic target for various diseases. Materials & methods: The authors developed and validated a 3D pharmacophore model of IKKbeta inhibitors via the HypoGen algorithm in Discovery Studio 2019, then performed virtual screening, molecular docking and kinase assays to identify hit compounds from the ChemDiv database. The compound with the highest inhibitory activity was further evaluated in adjuvant-induced arthritis rat models. Results: Among the four hit compounds, Hit 4 had the highest IKKbeta inhibitory activity (IC(50) = 30.4 +/- 3.8), and it could significantly ameliorate joint inflammation and damage in vivo. Conclusion: The identified compound, Hit 4, can be optimized as a therapeutic agent for inflammatory diseases.
