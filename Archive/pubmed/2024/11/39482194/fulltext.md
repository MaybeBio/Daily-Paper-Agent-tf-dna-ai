Gene expression regulation in hypoxic tumor microenvironments is mediated by O(2) responsive transcription factors (O(2)R-TFs), fine-tuning cancer cell metabolic demand for O(2) according to its availability. Here, we discuss key O(2)R-TFs and emerging artificial intelligence (AI)-based applications suitable for the interrogation of O(2)R-TF relationships specifying cancer cell metabolic adaptations to hypoxia.
