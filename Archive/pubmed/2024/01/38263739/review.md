### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that PNV binds to ct-DNA through groove binding, based on fluorescence spectroscopy, UV-visible absorption spectroscopy, and molecular modeling.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract text; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract provides no quantitative data, such as binding constants, fluorescence quenching constants, or UV-visible spectral shifts, to support the groove binding mode. The claim is stated qualitatively without reference to specific experimental results or docking scores.  
- **Why it matters** Groove binding is a specific molecular interaction mode that requires robust evidence, such as competitive displacement assays with known groove binders, ionic strength dependence, or detailed docking analysis. Without such evidence, the claim is unsupported and cannot be distinguished from other binding modes, such as intercalation or electrostatic interaction.  
- **Resolution test** Provide quantitative binding parameters, competitive displacement data with a known groove binder (e.g., Hoechst 33258), and docking scores with binding site analysis in the full manuscript.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that circular dichroism measurements showed a slight change in DNA structure, affirming groove binding.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract text; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract does not describe the nature or magnitude of the circular dichroism changes, nor how these changes specifically support groove binding over other modes. The interpretation of circular dichroism data in the context of drug-DNA interactions is complex and requires careful analysis of spectral features.  
- **Why it matters** Circular dichroism can indicate conformational changes in DNA, but such changes are not unique to groove binding. Without specific spectral details and comparison to controls, the conclusion is not justified.  
- **Resolution test** Include circular dichroism spectra with baseline and control experiments, and provide a detailed interpretation linking the observed changes to groove binding.

- **Concern ID** R1-M3  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that fluorescence quenching results suggested static quenching in the spontaneous development of the HSA-PNV complex through hydrophobic forces.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract text; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract provides no quantitative data, such as Stern-Volmer constants, quenching rate constants, or thermodynamic parameters (e.g., enthalpy and entropy changes), to support the static quenching mechanism and the role of hydrophobic forces. The claim of spontaneity is not supported by Gibbs free energy values.  
- **Why it matters** Static quenching versus dynamic quenching requires distinction, typically via temperature-dependent studies or lifetime measurements. The nature of the binding forces requires thermodynamic analysis. Without these data, the claims are speculative.  
- **Resolution test** Provide Stern-Volmer plots at multiple temperatures, fluorescence lifetime data, and thermodynamic parameters (ΔG, ΔH, ΔS) in the full manuscript.

- **Concern ID** R1-M4  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The authors claim that docking simulation results validated the findings of spectroscopic techniques.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract text; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract does not provide any docking scores, binding energies, or structural details of the predicted binding poses. The claim of validation is therefore unsubstantiated.  
- **Why it matters** Docking results can support experimental findings only if the computational parameters, scoring functions, and predicted binding modes are presented and critically compared with experimental data. Without these details, the validation claim is not assessable.  
- **Resolution test** Include docking scores, binding site residues, and a comparison of predicted and experimentally derived binding modes in the full manuscript.
