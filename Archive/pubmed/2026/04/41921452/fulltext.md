To develop novel anti-leukemia therapies, a CNN model identified C191-0266 targeting METTL3, which exhibited potent anti-leukemia activity. It inhibited the proliferation of OCI-AML3, MOLM-13, and THP-1 cell lines with IC(50) values of 16.91 muM, 19.01 muM, and 24.99 muM respectively, showed strong METTL3 binding (K(D) = 7.28 muM) and enzymatic inhibition (IC(50) = 7.639 muM), enhanced Ara-C's cytotoxicity by suppressing Ara-C-induced protective autophagy, maintained stable binding to METTL3, regulated the NF-kappaB pathway to inhibit TNF-alpha overexpression in Ara-C-resistant cell lines, and had favorable pharmacokinetics and safety profiles via ADME assessments, thus holding promise as an anti-leukemia drug candidate.
