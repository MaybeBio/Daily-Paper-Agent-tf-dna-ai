### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** The field reached a critical inflection point in 2024-2025, measurable through three quantifiable criteria: mechanistic durability, delivery competence, and clinical validation.
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, criteria 1-3; location not provided
- **Concern** The abstract asserts that the field reached an inflection point based on three criteria, but the thresholds for these criteria appear arbitrary. The durability threshold of 450 cell divisions and 12 months, the delivery efficiency threshold of 50 percent, and the clinical validation criterion are presented without justification for why these specific values constitute an inflection point. No comparative data from earlier periods are provided to demonstrate that these thresholds were newly crossed in 2024-2025.
- **Why it matters** The central thesis of the review rests on this inflection point claim. If the criteria are not rigorously defined or benchmarked against prior progress, the argument becomes circular or subjective. Readers cannot assess whether the field genuinely reached a new phase or whether the authors selected thresholds post hoc to fit a narrative.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should provide a clear methodology for how these criteria and thresholds were selected, including comparative data from earlier periods. Ideally, the authors would show that these thresholds were not met before 2024-2025 and that they represent a meaningful qualitative shift rather than incremental progress.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** Mechanistic durability is demonstrated by silencing maintained across at least 450 cell divisions in vitro and at least 12 months in vivo without continued editor expression.
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, criterion 1; location not provided
- **Concern** The durability data are presented as established facts, but no experimental context is provided. The abstract does not specify which model systems, target genes, or editor platforms produced these results. The claim of "without continued editor expression" is particularly important for the reversibility argument but is not substantiated with any experimental detail.
- **Why it matters** Durability without continued editor expression is the cornerstone of the authors' argument that epigenetic editing offers a distinct therapeutic advantage. If this claim is not rigorously supported, the entire premise of the review is weakened. The distinction between transient and durable silencing is central to the field's clinical relevance.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should provide specific citations to primary studies, including details on the experimental systems, target loci, editor constructs, and methods used to confirm the absence of continued editor expression. Ideally, the authors would include a summary table of durability studies with their key parameters.

- **Concern ID** R1-M3
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** Yes
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency
- **Claim pointer** Delivery competence is demonstrated by tissue-selective transduction at greater than 50 percent efficiency in liver, muscle, and whole brain via engineered lipid nanoparticle and AAV platforms.
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, criterion 2; location not provided
- **Concern** The delivery efficiency claim of greater than 50 percent across three tissue types is presented without specifying which studies, which animal models, which delivery vehicles, or which readouts were used. The claim of "whole brain" transduction is particularly striking and would require substantial evidence. The abstract does not distinguish between different cell types within these tissues or address potential off-target transduction.
- **Why it matters** Delivery efficiency is a critical determinant of clinical viability. If the 50 percent threshold is not consistently achieved across studies or is measured in ways that are not clinically relevant, the criterion loses meaning. The "whole brain" claim in particular raises questions about the specificity and uniformity of transduction.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should provide a comprehensive summary of delivery studies, including animal models, delivery vehicle specifications, transduction efficiency measurement methods, and consistency across studies. The authors should also address whether these efficiencies translate to clinically relevant levels of target gene silencing.

- **Concern ID** R1-M4
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** No
- **Axis** Claim precision
- **Claim pointer** Artificial intelligence contributes at distinct levels: deep learning platforms have directly accelerated clinical-stage LNP formulation screening and AAV capsid prediction; AlphaFold3 has optimized protein-DNA interaction validation without yet entering clinical programs; and the 2025 de novo design of DNA-binding proteins smaller than 65 amino acids represents a proof-of-concept breakthrough with zero clinical precedent.
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, AI contributions section; location not provided
- **Concern** The tiered assessment of AI contributions is useful, but the claims are vague. The abstract does not specify which deep learning platforms were used, how they accelerated LNP screening or AAV capsid prediction, or what specific outcomes resulted. The AlphaFold3 claim is similarly underspecified. The de novo design of DNA-binding proteins is presented as a breakthrough, but the abstract does not explain what these proteins do or how they would be used in epigenetic editing.
- **Why it matters** The AI section is a distinctive contribution of this review, but vague claims undermine its value. Readers cannot assess the maturity or reliability of AI contributions without specific examples and outcomes. The distinction between realized and anticipated contributions is important but requires concrete illustrations.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should provide specific examples of AI applications, including platform names, training data, validation methods, and outcomes. The authors should clearly distinguish between AI contributions that have been validated in published studies and those that are speculative or in development.

- **Concern ID** R1-M5
- **Severity** Major
- **Blocking** No
- **Axis** Logical consistency
- **Claim pointer** Epigenetic editing is progressively establishing itself as a distinctive therapeutic modality characterized by durable efficacy and sequence-independent safety, with reversibility as a theoretical safety mechanism validated preclinically, but the monitoring and intervention infrastructure required for its clinical implementation remains to be established.
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, concluding argument; location not provided
- **Concern** The abstract simultaneously claims that reversibility has been "validated preclinically" while stating that the "monitoring and intervention infrastructure required for its clinical implementation remains to be established." This creates a tension: if reversibility is validated, what exactly remains to be established? The abstract does not clarify whether the gap is in the biological mechanism, the clinical tools, or both. The phrase "sequence-independent safety" is also not defined or substantiated.
- **Why it matters** The authors' central argument about the therapeutic distinctiveness of epigenetic editing depends on the reversibility claim. If the abstract cannot clearly articulate what has been validated and what remains unproven, readers cannot assess the strength of the authors' position. The distinction between biological reversibility and clinical reversibility is critical but is not clearly drawn.
- **Resolution test** The full manuscript should clearly separate the biological evidence for reversibility from the clinical infrastructure needed to implement it. The authors should define "sequence-independent safety" and provide evidence for this claim. The concluding argument should be restructured to avoid the apparent contradiction.
