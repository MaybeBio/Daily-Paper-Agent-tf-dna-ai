Drug repurposing using smart nanocarriers may help overcome chemoresistance and poor tumor selectivity in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). Here, a pH/GSH-responsive dual chitosan/polylactic acid coated cobalt ferrite nanoparticle system (QL-CS/PLA-CFO NPs) has been explored to deliver QL for treating HCC. QL-CS/PLA-CFO NPs were prepared via ionotropic gelation and w/o/w emulsion with high loading (64.8%) and excellent stability (+28.0 mV). Comparative formulations, chitosan-only QL-CS-CFO (50.9%, -10.5 mV) and nonmagnetic QL-CS/PLA (60.7%, -21.0 mV), elucidated the roles of coating architecture and magnetic core. QL-CS/PLA-CFO NPs released 10.8% at pH 7.4 versus  approximately 95% at pH 4.5 with 10 mM GSH over 180 h, following quasi-Fickian diffusion kinetics. Encapsulation increased the WI38/HepG2 IC(5)(0) ratio from 1.12 to 1.93 through controlled endosomal release and protecting normal cells, with  approximately 1.2% hemolysis and enhanced DPPH antioxidants IC(5)(0) (47.9 vs. 71.9 mug/mL for free QL). DNA-binding studies revealed PLA-CFO synergy, yielding 38-fold enhanced affinity versus free QL through a minor-groove binding mode. Molecular docking suggested a potential interaction between quinidine and the HCC-associated biomarker glypican-3 (PDB 3GJB), supporting future investigation of its biological relevance. QL-CS/PLA-CFO NPs represent a promising platform for selective QL delivery in HCC, warranting further in vivo evaluation.
