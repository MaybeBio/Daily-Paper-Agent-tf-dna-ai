# Structural basis for P-TEFb association with BRD4


## SIGNIFICANCE STATEMENT
  The exquisite control of gene expression is required for proper development and cellular response to environmental stress. Positive transcription elongation factor b (P-TEFb) is required for the phosphorylation of RNA Pol II and leads to the coordinated release of RNA Pol II from pause sites near the promoter. P-TEFb is bound to bromo-domain containing protein 4 (BRD4), which stimulates P-TEFb activity; however, it is unknown how BRD4 associates with P-TEFb. Here we report a cryo-EM structure of P-TEFb bound to BRD4, revealing how BRD4 interacts with P-TEFb. Comparison of our structure with previously reported P-TEFb structures shows how P-TEFb is regulated by different transcription factor binding partners.
