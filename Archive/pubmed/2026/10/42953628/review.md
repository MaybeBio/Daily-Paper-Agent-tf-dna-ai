- **Shared manuscript claim summary** The authors present 3M-BSIP, a multimodal deep learning framework that predicts DNA-binding interfaces and residue-level base preferences for DNA-binding proteins (DBPs) from protein structure. The model integrates a protein language model, a graph neural network, and a surface point-cloud encoder via cross-attention. Claims include state-of-the-art performance on interface and base-preference prediction, robustness to computationally predicted and apo structures, and successful experimental validation in two applications: predicting a bacterial transcription factor binding motif and reprogramming the specificity of a restriction enzyme.
