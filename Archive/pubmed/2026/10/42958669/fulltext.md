# Jianpi Huayu decoction (健脾化瘀方) induces hepatocellular carcinoma apoptosis through flavonoid components targeting tumor protein p53 activation and serine-threonine protein kinase 1/B-cell lymphoma 2 inhibition: a network pharmacology-based study with experimental validation


## OBJECTIVE:
  To investigate the bioactive components and molecular mechanisms by which Jianpi Huayu decoction (健脾化瘀方, JHD) promotes apoptosis in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).


## METHODS:
  A multi-omics strategy integrating serum pharmacochemistry, metabolomics, network analysis, molecular docking, and in vitro validation was employed. Serum-absorbed components were identified from JHD-treated rats. Core targets and pathways were analyzed viaprotein-protein interaction (PPI) networks and kyoto encyclopedia of genes and genomes (KEGG) pathway enrichment. The effects of key candidate components (apigenin, naringin, daidzein, curzerenone, curcumenol) were validated in human hepatoma G2 cell line cells through cell viability, apoptosis, and protein expression assays.


## RESULTS:
  Fifty-one serum-absorbed JHD compounds were identified, comprising eight alkaloids, fifteen flavonoids, nine terpenoids, three quinones, and three phenylpropanoids. Apigenin, naringin, and daidzein demonstrated the highest bioavailability. Network analysis identified tumor protein 53 (TP53), serine-threonine kinase 1 (AKT1), B-cell lymphoma 2 (BCL2), epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR), and Jun proto-oncogene (JUN) as core targets, which converge on major cancer-related pathways, including phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)-serine-threonine kinase (AKT), mitogen-activated protein kinases (MAPK), and Janus kinase (JAK)-signal transducer and activator of transcription 3 (STAT3). Molecular docking revealed stable binding of naringin to AKT1 [binding energy: －12.6 kcal/mol; root mean square deviation (RMSD): 0.2-0.4 nm]. Apigenin showed the strongest binding affinity for TP53 (－8.2 kcal/mol) and AKT1 (－9.6 kcal/mol). In vitro, apigenin [half maximal inhibitory concentration (IC50) = 48.2 μM at 24 h] significantly upregulated TP53 (P < 0.01) while downregulating BCL2 (P < 0.05), AKT1 (P < 0.01), JUN (P < 0.0001), and EGFR (P < 0.01). Both naringin and curzerenone downregulated AKT1 and BCL2 at 48 h (P < 0.05). In contrast, curcumenol (a structural analog of curzerenone) exhibited no significant regulatory effects (P > 0.05).


## CONCLUSION:
  JHD promotes HCC apoptosis primarily viaits flavonoid-enriched components, notably apigenin and naringin, which function by activating TP53 and inhibiting the AKT1/BCL2 signaling axis. This study establishes a "component-target-pathway" framework for optimizing herbal formulations and bridges Traditional Chinese Medicine with precision oncology for flavonoid-based HCC therapy.
