# In silico analysis based on network pharmacology and biomolecular informatics to explore the mechanism of action of Erjing Pills (from Shengji Zonglu) in the treatment of leukotrichia


## Abstract
  This study aimed to explore the core active ingredients and potential molecular mechanisms of Erjing Pills, a prescription in the classic work of Traditional Chinese Medicine, “Shengji Zonglu,” in the treatment of leukotrichia by utilizing network pharmacology and biomolecular docking techniques. The chemical components and potential targets of Chinese herbal medicines were analyzed through databases such as the Traditional Chinese Medicine Systems Pharmacology Database. The targets related to leukotrichia were collected using GeneCards. The intersection targets were obtained using RStudio. The protein-protein interaction (PPI) network map and the “drug-component-target-disease” visualization network were generated using Cytoscape and STRING to screen the core components and key targets. Gene Ontology and Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes enrichment analyses were carried out using the Database for Annotation, Visualization and Integrated Discovery and RStudio. Finally, molecular docking verification was performed by AutoDock and PyMOL (Schrödinger LLC). The key active ingredients of Erjing Pills in the treatment of leukotrichia are β-sitosterol, quercetin, baicalein, and stigmasterol. The top 5 PPI core target proteins, in order, are AKT serine/threonine kinase 1, interleukin 6, tumor protein p53, cysteine-aspartic acid protease 3, and interleukin 1 beta. The Gene Ontology enrichment analysis suggests that the biological processes mainly include responses to exogenous stimuli, membrane rafts, and DNA-binding transcription factor binding. The Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes pathways involve signal pathways such as lipid and atherosclerosis, hepatitis B, Kaposi sarcoma virus infection, chemical carcinogenesis, and human cytomegalovirus infection. The molecular docking results indicate that most of the main active ingredients in Erjing Pills have relatively stable binding activities with the key targets, such as AKT serine/threonine kinase 1, interleukin 6, tumor protein p53, cysteine-aspartic acid protease 3, and interleukin 1 beta, in the PPI network. The active ingredients of Erjing Pills may interfere with the pathological process of leukotrichia by regulating key targets and signal pathways. This study provides a theoretical basis for the clinical application of Erjing Pills and indicates the direction for subsequent experimental research.


## 1. Introduction
  Leukotrichia, a common pigment-reducing disease of scalp hair, is one of the earliest and most obvious indicators of human aging.[1] Its research background and current situation have received much attention. With the acceleration of the modern life rhythm and the increase of environmental pressure, the prevalence rate of leukotrichia is rising year by year, becoming one of the important factors affecting people’s appearance and mental health.[2] Leukotrichia is an excellent model for studying human pigmentation and aging in affected micro-organs. Studies have shown that hair turning white is the result of the combined action of multiple factors, such as oxidative damage,[3] melanocyte aging,[3] insufficient pigment production,[3] DNA damage,[4] and excessive mTORC1 activity.[5] Genetic factors, metabolic disorders, clock genes, signal transduction, neurotransmitters, and other regulatory factors are also involved in this process.[6] This leads to reduced melanin production, defective melanosome transfer, and depletion of melanocytes in the hair follicle pigment unit. Irreversible damage occurs when the melanocyte stem cells are also depleted.

  As a treasure of the Chinese nation, traditional Chinese medicine has accumulated rich experience in the treatment of leukotrichia. Among them, Erjing Pills in “Shengji Zonglu · Volume 198” is a traditional Chinese medicine prescription with unique advantages in regulating the essence, qi, and spirit of the human body and promoting hair pigmentation. However, although traditional Chinese medicine has remarkable curative effects in the treatment of leukotrichia, its mechanism of action and material basis of pharmacodynamics still need to be further elucidated. Network pharmacology and molecular docking technology, as important tools in modern biomedical research, can reveal the complex relationships between drugs and diseases at the systems and network levels, providing new ideas and methods for the modernization of traditional Chinese medicine research.[7–12] This study aims to deeply explore the material basis and mechanism of action of Erjing Pills in the treatment of leukotrichia through network pharmacology and molecular docking technology, providing a scientific basis for the modernization research and clinical application of traditional Chinese medicine. This not only helps to reveal the scientific connotation of traditional Chinese medicine but also provides new strategies and methods for the treatment of leukotrichia, which has important theoretical and practical significance.


## 2. Materials and methods

### 2.1. Screening of active compounds and their potential targets of Erjing Pills
  In this study, the chemical constituents and potential targets of the 2 Chinese medicinal materials, Polygonatum sibiricum and Lycium barbarum, in Erjing Pills were collected using the Traditional Chinese Medicine Systems Pharmacology Database (https://www.tcmsp-e.com/load_intro.php?id=40). The compounds of P sibiricum and L barbarum were screened through the ADME (absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion) model, and compounds with oral bioavailability ≥30% and drug-likeness ≥0.18 were selected[13] to ensure good oral absorption and application effects. In addition, these active ingredients were imported into the PubChem database (https://pubChem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) to obtain their structural information. For each active ingredient, target prediction was carried out under the condition of “Homo sapiens” through the Swiss Target Prediction database (http://www.swisstargetprediction.ch/) to ensure the integrity of the compound targets, and the target names were standardized using the UniProt database (https://www.uniprot.org/).


### 2.2. Screening of potential targets of leukotrichia
  Targets related to leukotrichia were searched in databases such as GeneCards (https://www.genecards.org/) using keywords such as “leukotrichia,” “gray hair,” “hair graying,” and “canities.” At the same time, the R4.4.1 (R Core Team) programming language was used with the VennDiagram package to analyze and identify the common targets of leukotrichia and Erjing Pills as the potential targets of Erjing Pills in the treatment of leukotrichia.


### 2.3. Construction of protein-protein interaction (PPI) network and screening of key targets
  In the STRING database (http://stringdb.org), a PPI network composed of the common targets of Erjing Pills and leukotrichia was constructed, and the organism was set to “Homo sapiens.” Subsequently, the network was extracted and analyzed in Cytoscape 3.10.3 software (Institute for Systems Biology, https://www.cytoscape.org/). The network was enriched and analyzed using degree value, betweenness value, and closeness value in the CytoNCA plugin to identify the relationships among key targets. Visualization processing was carried out through “Generate style from statistics” in “Tools.” The size and color depth of nodes reflected the degree values, and the thickness and color shade of edges reflected the binding score. Finally, nodes with high degree values were selected as the putative key targets in the PPI network. It is known that the degree, betweenness, and closeness parameters are 3 important network analysis parameters, and nodes with these 3 parameters higher than the corresponding median values can be used as the main nodes.[14]

  The active ingredients of Erjing Pills were explored by analyzing the medicinal material–target network using Cytoscape 3.10.3 software. At the same time, the PPI network was analyzed using Cytoscape 3.10.3 software to obtain information on key targets. To reliably illustrate the relationships among the active ingredients, common targets, and Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG) pathways, Cytoscape software was used to analyze the medicinal material–component–target–pathway network. Based on this network, the key pharmacological mechanisms of Erjing Pills in the treatment of leukotrichia were identified using 3 network parameters.


### 2.4. Gene Ontology (GO) and KEGG pathway enrichment analysis
  GO and KEGG enrichment analyses were performed on the action targets through the DAVID database (https://davidbioinformatics.nih.gov/ in the manuscript), and visualization was carried out using the barplot package in RStudio 4.4.1. With P < .05 as the screening condition, the key targets involved in biological processes (BP), molecular functions (MF), cellular components (CC), and signal pathways were analyzed.


### 2.5. Molecular docking
  The key active ingredients were searched in the PubChem database, and the chemical structures were drawn in ChemDraw, transferred to ChemBio 3D to generate 3-dimensional chemical structures, and conformational optimization was carried out using “Calculation-MM2-Minimize Energy-Run,” and finally saved as structure data file format files; the compounds were converted into Protein Data Bank (PDB) file format using PyMOL 3.0.4 software. The crystal structures of the core targets were obtained from the PDB (http://www.rcsb.org/).

  The AutoDock tool (http://mgltools.scripps.edu/) was used to remove water molecules and add hydrogen atoms and charges to the crystal structures of the core targets, which were then saved in PDB partial charge & atom type format. Molecular docking between the key active ingredients and the core targets was performed using AutoDock, and the binding mode with the lowest binding energy was selected for further analysis. The interaction between the bioactive compounds and the core targets was visualized as a 3-dimensional graph using PyMOL 3.0.4.


## 3. Results

### 3.1. Screening results of the active ingredients of Erjing Pills
  A total of 226 chemical constituents of the compound Erjing Pills were retrieved through the Traditional Chinese Medicine Systems Pharmacology Database, including 38 from P sibiricum and 188 from L barbarum. After screening with oral bioavailability ≥30% and drug-likeness ≥0.18 and combining and deleting duplicate values, 57 compounds were obtained, including 12 from P sibiricum and 45 from L barbarum, such as saponins in P sibiricum and stigmasterol α1 in L barbarum. The main compound ID information is detailed in Table 1.


### 3.2. Common targets of Erjing Pills and leukotrichia
  After screening and deduplication through Excel (Microsoft Corporation) spreadsheets, a total of 187 targets related to Erjing Pills and 4979 targets related to leukotrichia were obtained. Analysis and visualization were performed using the VennDiagram package with the R software (version 4.4.1), and a total of 149 common targets of Erjing Pills and leukotrichia were obtained (see Fig. 1 for details).


### 3.3. PPI network of Erjing Pills in the treatment of leukotrichia
  The intersecting Venn gene data (IntersectGene) were imported into the STRING database. The biological species was set to “Homo sapiens,” the credibility was set to >0.4, and hidden free points were set. The obtained PPI network structure diagram is shown in Figure 2. There are a total of 149 nodes and 3030 edges. Then, the PPI network diagram data were analyzed using Cytoscape 3.10.3 software. Using the CytoNCA plugin, we calculated 3 topological parameters (degree, betweenness, closeness) for all nodes and screened nodes with all 3 parameters higher than the median (degree ≥ 35, betweenness ≥ 0.02, closeness ≥ 0.4) as core targets.[14] After filtering, the network yielded 93 nodes and 366 edges (see Fig. 3A). In this figure, nodes represent proteins, and edges represent PPI relationships. The larger the circle in the figure, the closer the connections generated by its node. Topological analysis[15] shows that the central targets include AKT serine/threonine kinase 1 (AKT1), interleukin 6 (IL6), tumor protein p53 (TP53), and cysteine-aspartic acid protease 3 (CASP3; see Fig. 3B). Furthermore, the “disease-target-active ingredient-drug” network structure was constructed. It can be seen that there are multiple active ingredients in a traditional Chinese medicine and that there are also active ingredients shared by multiple traditional Chinese medicines. In the correspondence between active compounds and targets, the phenomenon that 1 active compound acts on multiple targets and multiple active chemical components act on one target is widespread, which verifies that Erjing Pills act in a holistic and synergistic manner with pairs of drugs, multiple components, and multiple targets in the treatment of leukotrichia. Among them, the size of the degree value represents the size of the node and reflects the importance of the ingredient, target, and medicinal material.[13] By analyzing the degree values between active ingredients and targets, the top 5 active ingredients are MOL000098 (quercetin) with a degree value of 116; MOL002714 (baicalein) with a degree value of 27; MOL000358 (β-sitosterol) with a degree value of 24; MOL000449 (stigmasterol) with a degree value of 18; MOL000546 (saponin) with a degree value of 16. The top 5 action targets mainly involved by the active ingredients are progesterone receptor, nuclear receptor coactivator 2, nuclear receptor subfamily 3 group C member 2, prostaglandin-endoperoxide synthase 2, and PTGS1 (see Fig. 4 for details).


### 3.4. Results of biofunction enrichment analysis

#### 3.4.1. GO analysis results of potential targets
  The results of the GO analysis on potential targets are as follows: a total of 1345 BP, 27 CC, and 110 MF were identified. Among them, the BP mainly includes processes such as the response to exogenous biological stimuli, the response to molecules of bacterial origin, epithelial cell proliferation, the response to lipopolysaccharide, and wound healing. The CC consists of components such as membrane rafts, membrane microdomains, vesicle lumens, secretory granule lumens, and components of the RNA polymerase II transcriptional regulatory complex. The MF involves functions such as DNA-binding transcription factors, RNA polymerase II-specific binding, transcriptional activator activity, and ubiquitin-like protein ligase. Using P < .05 as the initial screening condition, the top 20 pathways with the highest count values were screened out, and then a visualization bar chart of the GO pathways was drawn using a graphing tool (see Fig. 5A–F). Figure 5A shows the bar chart of BP, and Figure 5B shows the bubble chart of BP. Figure 5C shows the bar chart of CC, and Figure 5D shows the bubble chart of CC. Figure 5E shows the bar chart of MF, and Figure 5F shows the bubble chart of MF.


#### 3.4.2. KEGG analysis results
  The KEGG analysis results show that a total of 166 pathways were obtained, involving lipid and atherosclerosis, hepatitis B, Kaposi sarcoma-associated herpesvirus infection, chemical carcinogenesis-receptor activation, and human cytomegalovirus infection. Using P < .05 as the initial screening condition, the top 20 pathways with the highest Count values were screened out. Bubble charts of the KEGG pathways were prepared (see Fig. 6A–C specifically). Figure 6B is the bar chart of the KEGG analysis, Figure 6 shows the bubble chart of the KEGG analysis, and Figure 7 is the signal pathway diagram of lipid and atherosclerosis (KEGG pathway: HSA05417).


### 3.5. Molecular docking
  The crystal structures of AKT1 (PDB ID: 1H10), IL6 (PDB ID: 1ALU), TP53 (PDB ID: 7BWN), CASP3 (PDB ID: 1GFW), and interleukin 1 beta (interleukin-1 beta [IL1B]; PDB ID: 1HIB) were obtained from the PDB. AutoDockTools software (Version 1.5.7; Scripps Research Institute, available at http://autodock.scripps.edu/) was used to set up the graphical user interface program for the AutoDock software to prepare for the docking procedure. A docking grid box with a maximum size of 126 × 126 × 126 Å was defined, and the grid point spacing was set to 0.375 Å. The genetic algorithm in AutoDock 4.2.6 was employed for the docking calculations. In the docking simulation, each ligand was kept flexible, while the amino acid residues of the protein were kept rigid. AutoDock was run to perform the molecular docking operation, and the binding energy was obtained as a reference indicator. PyMOL (version 3.0.4, also available at http://www.pymol.org) was used to conduct visual analysis, and Discovery Studio software was used to visualize the interactions between the test compounds and the key residues (refer to Table 2 and Fig. 8 for the molecular binding energy). The results showed that, except for the docking between IL6 and baicalein, the binding energy results of the other 24 dockings were all less than or equal to −4.25 kcal/mol, indicating that the ligands and receptors had a certain binding activity,[16] which demonstrated good docking activity between Erjing Pills and the targets. The binding energy of TP53-diosgenin suggested a strong interaction between them. However, due to the small molecule structure, there were fewer docking oxygen atoms, and the docking distance was >3.5, resulting in a low possibility of forming hydrogen-bond interactions. There was no hydrogen-bond interaction between CASP3 and diosgenin, which had the second-highest binding force. Therefore, TP53 and quercetin, which had the top 5 binding energies, were selected as the docking binding mode for visual reference. The binding mode of the active sites of TP53 and quercetin is shown in the figure. Binding energy is the energy released when several particles combine from a free state into a composite particle, usually with a negative value. The larger the value, the more stable the molecular structure. Generally, if the binding energy is less than −4.25 kcal/mol, it is considered that the ligand and receptor have a certain binding activity; if it is less than −5.0 kcal/mol, there is good binding activity; if it is less than −7.25 kcal/mol, there is strong binding activity. The binding energy of TP53 and quercetin is −8.8 kcal/mol, indicating a strong interaction between them. Quercetin binds within the hydrophobic cavity of TP53, and Glu95 and Gln248 of TP53 form 2 hydrogen bonds with quercetin, maintaining the stability of the complex. Gln183, Ar96, Tyr182, Thr97, Gln184, Lys162, Leu247, and Thr246 of TP53 also have hydrophobic interactions with quercetin (see Fig. 9 for details). Notably, TP53 showed strong binding activity with 2 key components: diosgenin (−10.7 kcal/mol) and quercetin (−8.8 kcal/mol), both below the −7.25 kcal/mol threshold for “strong binding,” indicating the most stable ligand-receptor interactions (Table 3).


## 4. Discussion
  Leukotrichia is relatively common among middle-aged and elderly people. However, in recent years, the incidence rate has also been gradually increasing among young people. Its etiology is rather complex and may be related to factors such as genetics, environment, and lifestyle.[17,18] At present, there are no specific drugs for the treatment of leukotrichia. It can only be prevented and alleviated by advocating a healthy lifestyle, such as maintaining a balanced diet, ensuring sufficient sleep, and engaging in moderate physical exercise. Meanwhile, exposure to harmful environments should be avoided.[19] The purpose of this study is to explore the potential mechanism of Erjing Pills in the treatment of leukotrichia, which may involve methods related to signal pathways, and to provide new ideas for the clinical treatment of leukotrichia.

  Erjing Pills originated from “Shengji Zonglu Volume 198.” In clinical applications, it is commonly used to treat various diseases caused by liver and kidney insufficiency and deficiency of essence and blood, such as vision decline, weakness of the waist and knees, dizziness, and tinnitus. In addition, Erjing Pills also have antiaging and antidepression effects.[20,21] Its main components are P sibiricum and L barbarum, which have the functions of tonifying qi and blood, benefiting the liver and kidney, and nourishing yin and moistening dryness. P sibiricum exerts antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cell-repairing effects through its rich chemical components such as polysaccharides and saponins.[22,23] L barbarum, through L barbarum polysaccharide and betaine, can not only exert antioxidant and anti-inflammatoryeffects but also reduce blood lipid, resist fibrosis, and inhibit angiogenesis, having preventive and therapeutic effects on various diseases.[24] Studies have shown that L barbarum polysaccharide, mediated by microRNA-124, has a strong ability to resist the inflammatory damage of ATDC5 induced by IL-1β. It can effectively reduce the expression levels of cyclooxygenase-2-2 and IL6 and simultaneously inhibit the activity of signaling pathways such as c-Jun N-terminal kinase. Betaine has anti-inflammatory, antihypertensive, anti-fibrotic, and anti-angiogenic characteristics.[25–28]

  In this study, the effective chemical components of P sibiricum and L barbarum in Erjing Pills, such as saponins and sitosterol α1, were first screened, and the corresponding target proteins were identified. Meanwhile, with the help of gene databases, the target proteins related to leukotrichia were explored. Using RStudio 4.4.1, 149 common targets of Erjing Pills and leukotrichia were successfully obtained. Second, by using various auxiliary tool software, the PPI network and drug-active ingredient-protein target network of Erjing Pills and leukotrichia were constructed. It was found that the main effective components of Erjing Pills in regulating leukotrichia include quercetin, β-sitosterol, baicalein, stigmasterol, and saponins. These effective components may play a therapeutic role through multiple pathways, such as regulating lipid metabolism, neurotransmitter release, antioxidation, and inhibiting inflammatory reactions. Among them, quercetin significantly reduces the oxidative stress damage of body cells with its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activities.[29] β-sitosterol plays an important role in regulating cholesterol metabolism and maintaining cell membrane stability.[30] Baicalein exhibits anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective properties and effectively inhibits the occurrence of inflammation. Stigmasterol also shows certain effects of antioxidation, anti-inflammation, and reducing the cholesterol level in the body.[31] Saponins exhibit extensive pharmacological potential with their anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antitumor functions.[32]

  Through further research, it was discovered that each component in Erjing Pills may affect the intracellular signal transduction pathways by regulating the activities of multiple core targets. For example, topological analysis revealed that among the top 5 core targets, AKT1 is involved in processes such as cell survival, proliferation, and metabolism.[33] The regulation of its activity may be closely related to the therapeutic effects of Erjing Pills. IL6 plays an important role in the inflammatory response.[34] The regulation of IL6 by Erjing Pills may help to alleviate the inflammatory symptoms of the body and may contribute to the treatment of leukotrichia. TP53, as an important tumor suppressor factor,[35] may have changes in its activity that are related to the antitumor and other effects of Erjing Pills, which have certain guiding significance for inhibiting leukotrichia caused by tumor radiotherapy and chemotherapy. Additionally, CASP3 is also involved in the process of cell apoptosis.[36] The regulation of CASP3 by the effective components of Erjing Pills will also affect the cell apoptosis mechanism. IL1B plays a crucial role in the inflammatory and immune responses. Regulating IL1B may contribute to the regulation of the immune system function.[37] These research results provide important clues for in-depth exploration of the mechanism of action of Erjing Pills and also lay a certain theoretical foundation for its clinical application. Future research can further explore the specific regulatory mechanisms of Erjing Pills on these core targets and its therapeutic effects in other diseases to achieve the treatment of different diseases with the same method. In addition, it is also possible to consider using Erjing Pills in combination with other drugs in order to enhance the therapeutic effect.

  The GO analysis results suggest that Erjing Pills may be involved in a variety of BP, such as RNA polymerase II-specific binding and DNA-binding transcription factor binding, and may involve multiple molecular mechanisms. That is, Erjing Pills may affect the binding activity of DNA-binding transcription factors by regulating the specific expression of genes and regulating the transcription process of related genes. In addition, for the biological process of ubiquitin-like protein ligase binding,[38] it is also worthy of in-depth exploration to investigate the possibility that Erjing Pills affect the ubiquitination modification process and regulate the stability and function of proteins. Future research can further explore the specific mechanism of action of Erjing Pills in these BP and its synergistic effect with other drugs or treatment methods, providing a more in-depth theoretical basis for clinical applications.

  The KEGG enrichment analysis revealed that Erjing Pills can regulate organic system pathways, especially those of lipid metabolism and the process of atherosclerotic signaling pathways. The lipid metabolism pathway involves receptor signaling pathways such as those of AKT, IL6, and CASP3, suggesting that Erjing Pills may participate in the regulation process of sebum metabolism by modulating signaling pathways like AKT1 and IL6, suppressing the inflammatory response, and affecting the balance of sebum secretion on the scalp, thereby influencing the hair metabolism process and acting on the treatment of leukotrichia. This provides new clues for in-depth understanding of the mechanism of action of Erjing Pills and also potential targets for the treatment of related diseases. Future research can further explore the specific ways in which Erjing Pills act in these signaling pathways and its long-term effects on sebum dissolution. Multiple tumor-related pathways appeared in the enrichment results, indicating that Erjing Pills may inhibit cell proliferation, prevent hyperkeratosis of the sebaceous gland ducts, and improve hair follicles. Notably, pathways such as hepatitis B, Kaposi sarcoma virus infection, and human cytomegalovirus infection though seemingly unrelated to leukotrichia – may contribute to pathological progression via shared mechanisms: chronic viral infection activates the nuclear factor kappa-B pathway to promote IL6/IL-1β release, which inhibits tyrosinase activity in hair follicle melanocytes (HMCs)[3,19]; chemical carcinogens in the environment (e.g., polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons) induce TP53 activation via DNA damage, accelerating HMC apoptosis.[4,23] However, some pathways may also be artifacts of the “wide-target effect” of network pharmacology (i.e., nonspecific binding of Erjing Pills components to proteins in these pathways), which should be interpreted with caution. Additionally, molecular docking showed that there is mostly a good binding force between the core active components and macromolecular proteins, and they may be the potential active components and targets through which Erjing Pills act on leukotrichia.

  The above analysis further indicates that Erjing Pills may exert their pharmacological effects by regulating these signaling pathways. In addition, in vivo experiments can be considered to verify the reliability of results such as those from KEGG analysis and further evaluate the therapeutic potential of Erjing Pills. Meanwhile, analyzing the chemical components of Erjing Pills is helpful for revealing its active components and their target sites of action. Future research can also focus on the combined application of Erjing Pills with other drugs to explore more effective treatment strategies.

  In summary, Erjing Pills can effectively improve the pathological state of leukotrichia through multi-target and multi-pathway regulatory effects. It may participate in the regulation of sebum metabolism, reduce the level of cell proliferation, improve the hyperkeratosis of hair follicles and sebaceous gland ducts, and even promote the functional recovery of melanocytes and the regeneration of pigments. It is clear that most of the core components used have a very good binding to the targets.[39] In-depth research on these action mechanisms will provide a scientific basis for the clinical application of Erjing Pills in leukotrichia. However, its application still faces some limitations. The application of data fusion technology in traditional Chinese medicine research mainly focuses on the fusion of chemical substance information, and relatively few studies have been conducted on the fusion of other types of data such as clinical data. In addition, the application of data fusion technology is also affected by factors such as data quality, data standardization, and model selection. Furthermore, some KEGG pathways (e.g., Kaposi sarcoma virus infection) may be false positives due to the “wide-target effect” of network pharmacology, and their direct relevance to leukotrichia requires further verification. Finally, this study is limited to in silico predictions; future research should include in vitro experiments, such as culturing human HMCs to verify the effect of quercetin (key component) on TP53/AKT1 expression and melanin synthesis under oxidative stress, and in vivo experiments, such as establishing a D-galactose-induced leukotrichia model in C57BL/6 mice to detect changes in hair grayness, follicle melanocyte count, and IL6/AKT1 pathway activity after Erjing Pills intervention. Therefore, in the future, experimental verification and clinical data verification should be further carried out on the basis of simulating the interaction between small molecule ligands and macromolecular receptors to further reveal the binding mode and action mechanism between the active components of traditional Chinese medicine in Erjing Pills and the targets, hoping to provide new ideas and methods for the modern research of traditional Chinese medicine.
