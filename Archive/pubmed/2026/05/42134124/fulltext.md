A peptide fraction (A1) with molecular weight < 10 kDa was prepared from Laetiporus sulphureus via enzymatic hydrolysis and ultrafiltration. A1, primarily containing low-molecular-weight peptides (63.59% <5 kDa, 49.63% <3 kDa), exhibited strong in vitro antioxidant activity. In H(2)O(2)-injured CAL-27 cells, A1 reduced reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels, restored mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP). Furthermore, it further activated the Nrf2/HO-1 pathway, leading to increased activities of the superoxide dismutase (SOD) and glutathione peroxidase (GSH-Px), thereby protecting against oxidative damage. It also promoted cell migration and wound closure. Peptidomic analysis identified 1857 peptides, predominantly 3-7 amino acids long. Five novel tripeptides (MRF, RMF, PRW, RMW, and MDW) showed high binding affinity to Nrf2/HO-1 pathway proteins in silico. These findings demonstrate the antioxidant and cytoprotective potential of L. sulphureus peptides as functional food components against oxidative stress.
