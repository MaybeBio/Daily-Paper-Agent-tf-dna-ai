Accurate inference of gene regulatory networks (GRNs) from single-cell RNA-seq data remains challenging due to the complexity of transcription factor (TF)-target interactions and the difficulty in capturing both local and global regulatory dependencies. Here, we present GeneKAN, a novel deep learning framework that integrates multiscale graph convolution with Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs) for robust GRN inference. GeneKAN employs a kernel-based graph convolution module to hierarchically encode gene representations, while KANs model high-order, interpretable TF-target relationships through learnable spline activations. Benchmarking against eleven existing models, and seven scRNA-seq datasets from BEELINE, including diverse cell types, species, and ground-truth networks (ChIP-seq, STRING, LOF/GOF), demonstrates that our proposed GeneKAN achieves state-of-the-art performance in both AUROC and AUPRC. Ablation studies of the proposed model confirm the necessity of each component. Biological validation on human hepatocyte and lung cancer datasets reveals that GeneKAN not only recovers known regulatory interactions with high precision but also identifies functionally relevant hub genes and oncogenic pathways. GeneKAN thus offers a powerful, interpretable, and scalable solution for GRN reconstruction under real-world conditions of data sparsity and noise, with significant potential for advancing systems biology and precision medicine.
