Plant Promoter Analysis Navigator (PlantPAN) 5.0 (https://plantpan.itps.ncku.edu.tw/) is an integrated multiomics resource for investigating plant cis-regulatory elements and transcriptional regulatory networks. This release expands genome annotations and promoter sequences to 240 species and provides 71 040 transcription factor (TF) annotations and 7762 nonredundant binding matrices. Its companion database, PCBase 3.0, substantially expands ChIP-seq data and newly incorporates ATAC-seq, bisulfite-sequencing, and four Hi-C-based chromatin interaction datasets for Arabidopsis thaliana. The new Differential Analysis function enables comparison of TF binding, chromatin accessibility, and DNA methylation across conditions. The redesigned Promoter Analysis interface provides TF family-level statistics, with a Pattern Overview panel integrating TF-binding site (TFBS) densities, plant DNA large language model-predicted open-chromatin regions, CpG-rich regions, and tandem repeats. Two new modules are introduced: (i) Transcriptome Regulatory Analysis and Coexpression, where candidate regulatory TFs and shared cis-elements in gene sets are prioritized through differential expression and coexpression analysis, and (ii) Interaction Topology Analysis for Chromatin and TFs, where chromatin interaction regions are integrated to reveal potentially coregulated genes and their shared TFs and predicted TFBSs. A Dockerized package enables large-scale local promoter analyses, with data privacy. Therefore, PlantPAN 5.0 is a comprehensive framework for discovering plant transcriptional regulatory mechanisms.
