### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract states that ASXL1 K351 monoubiquitination promotes nucleosomal H2AK119Ub deubiquitination by stabilizing the PR-DUB catalytic pocket, increasing V(max) without affecting K(m).  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The kinetic claim of increased V(max) with unchanged K(m) is presented without any numerical data, statistical analysis, or experimental context. No information is given on the enzyme preparation, ubiquitination method, substrate concentrations, or number of replicates.  
- **Why it matters** The distinction between V(max) and K(m) effects is central to the proposed mechanism of catalytic activation without altered substrate binding. Without supporting kinetic data, this claim cannot be evaluated.  
- **Resolution test** Provide full kinetic curves, fitted parameters with errors, and statistical comparisons between unmodified and monoubiquitinated PR-DUB. Include details on how ASXL1 K351 monoubiquitination was achieved and verified.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Structural evidence  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract claims that ubiquitin at ASXL1 K351 bridges the BAP1 and ASXL1 subunits, functioning as a cross-bracing glue that constrains their conformational dynamics without altering the nucleosome-binding interface.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The structural conclusion is stated definitively, but no structural data are presented. It is unclear whether this is based on a crystal structure, cryo-EM, crosslinking, or modeling. The claim that the nucleosome-binding interface is unaltered requires direct structural comparison.  
- **Why it matters** The "glue" mechanism is the central conceptual advance of the paper. If the structural evidence is indirect or incomplete, the conclusion would be overstated.  
- **Resolution test** Provide structural models or experimental data showing the ubiquitin position at K351 and its contacts with both BAP1 and ASXL1. Include evidence that the nucleosome-binding interface is unchanged upon ubiquitination.

- **Concern ID** R1-M3  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Dynamics evidence  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract states that molecular dynamics and hydrogen-deuterium exchange mass spectrometry revealed that this modification locks PR-DUB in a catalytic state poised for substrate cleavage.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The results from MD simulations and HDX-MS are summarized without any quantitative details. No information is provided on simulation length, force field, system setup, or the specific regions showing altered dynamics. The HDX-MS data are not described in terms of peptide-level resolution or statistical significance.  
- **Why it matters** The claim that the enzyme is "locked" in a catalytic state is a strong dynamical statement. Without details on the methods and the magnitude of observed changes, this conclusion is not verifiable.  
- **Resolution test** Provide simulation parameters and convergence criteria, and show HDX-MS difference maps with statistical thresholds. Demonstrate that the observed dynamical changes correlate with the proposed catalytic state.

- **Concern ID** R1-M4  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Generalizability and specificity  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract proposes a "unique ubiquitin function of intersubunit fastening through a molecular glue effect" and a "unique ubiquitination-mediated regulatory paradigm."  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract only; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract does not provide evidence that this mechanism is unique or generalizable. No comparison is made to other ubiquitination sites on ASXL1 or other deubiquitinase complexes. The specificity of K351 is not demonstrated, and it is unclear whether other lysine residues could produce similar effects.  
- **Why it matters** The claim of uniqueness requires demonstration that the effect is specific to K351 and not a general property of ubiquitination at other sites. Without this, the conceptual advance may be overstated.  
- **Resolution test** Include experiments with other ASXL1 lysine mutants or other ubiquitination sites to show specificity. Discuss or test whether the mechanism applies to related PR-DUB complexes.
