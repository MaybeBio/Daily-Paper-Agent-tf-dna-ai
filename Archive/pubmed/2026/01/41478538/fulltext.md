ETHNOPHARMACOLOGICAL RELEVANCE: ZiYin XiFeng (ZYXF) Formula is a traditional prescription used for the treatment of brain disorders characterized by symptoms such as tremor and dizziness. In recent years, its potential therapeutic effects on Parkinson's disease (PD) have attracted increasing attention. However, the precise molecular mechanisms underlying the effects of ZYXF on PD remain insufficiently elucidated. AIM OF THE STUDY: This study aimed to explore the neuroprotective effects of ZYXF on PD and to elucidate its underlying molecular mechanisms. MATERIALS AND METHODS: In vivo and in vitro PD models were established using MPTP-induced mice and MPP(+)-treated SH-SY5Y cells. Behavioral assessments, histological analyses, biochemical assays, and transmission electron microscopy were employed to evaluate the neuroprotective and anti-ferroptotic effects of ZYXF. In addition, untargeted metabolomics, network pharmacology, and molecular docking were performed to identify potential targets. RESULTS: ZYXF significantly improved motor performance, preserved dopaminergic neurons, and alleviated ferroptosis-associated biochemical and ultrastructural abnormalities. Metabolomics revealed that ZYXF remodeled glutathione and phospholipid metabolism, restoring redox homeostasis. Network pharmacology identified Nrf2 as a key target, and molecular docking further confirmed strong binding affinities between representative ZYXF compounds and Nrf2. Mechanistically, ZYXF activated the Nrf2/SLC7A11/GPX4 pathway, reduced iron overload, and inhibited lipid peroxidation. Its protective effects were mimicked by the Nrf2 activator CDDO-Me and attenuated by the inhibitor ML385. CONCLUSION: ZYXF confers neuroprotection in PD by suppressing ferroptosis through activation of the Nrf2/SLC7A11/GPX4 pathway.
