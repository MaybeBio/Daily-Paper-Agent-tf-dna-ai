The seamless integration of laboratory experiments and detailed computational modeling provides an exciting route to uncovering many new insights into complex biological processes. In particular, the development of agent-based modeling using supercomputers has provided new opportunities for highly detailed, validated simulations that provide the researcher with greater understanding of these processes and new directions for investigation. This chapter examines some of the principles behind the powerful computational framework FLAME and its application in a number of different areas with a more detailed look at a particular signaling example involving the NF-kappaB cascade.
