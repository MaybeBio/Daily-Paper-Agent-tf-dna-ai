Ilex rotunda Thunb. has been used in traditional medicine for treating rheumatoid arthritis, relieving pain and indigestion. In the present study, we isolated three new caffeic acid benzyl ester (CABE) analogs (1-3) along with eight known compounds (4-11) from the extract of I. rotunda. The absolute configuration of alpha-hydoxycarboxylic acid in 1 was assigned with the phenylglycine methyl ester (PGME) method. We further investigated their anti-inflammatory activities in lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced macrophages (RAW 264.7) cells. Among them, compounds 2-4, 7, 8, 10, and 11 suppressed the production of nitric oxide (NO), pro-inflammatory mediators. It was additionally confirmed that the anti-inflammatory effect of active compound 2 was through significant suppression of cytokines, including interleukin (IL)-6, IL-1beta, tumor necrosis factor (TNF)-alpha, and IL-8 in LPS-stimulated RAW 264.7 cells and colon epithelial (HT-29) cells. Western blot analysis revealed that compound 2 decreased the LPS-induced expression of inducible nitric oxide synthase (iNOS), cyclooxygenase (COX-2), and phosphorylated extracellular regulated kinase (pERK)1/2. The following molecular docking simulations showed the significant interactions of compound 2 with the iNOS protein. These results suggested that the compound 2 can be used as potential candidate for treating inflammatory diseases such as inflammatory bowel disease (IBD).
