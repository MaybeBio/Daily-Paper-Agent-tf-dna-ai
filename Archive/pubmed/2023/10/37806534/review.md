### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence quality  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract claims that *C. nitida* infusion significantly reversed diabetes-induced changes in IRS-1, p53, PI3K, BCL2, and GLUT4 expression, and that results were statistically significant compared to metformin.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract Results section; location not provided  
- **Concern** No quantitative data are provided, such as fold changes, expression levels, variance measures, or p-values. The claim of statistical significance relative to metformin cannot be verified without these data.  
- **Why it matters** The central conclusion rests on the magnitude and statistical robustness of gene expression changes. Without numerical evidence, the claim is unsupported and cannot be evaluated for biological or clinical relevance.  
- **Resolution test** Provide a table or figure with mean expression values, standard errors or confidence intervals, and exact p-values for all comparisons, including the metformin group.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Methodological transparency  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract states that type 2 diabetic rats were administered *C. nitida* infusion at 150 and 300 mg/kg bodyweight, and that a high dose was given to a normal toxicological group.  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract Methods section; location not provided  
- **Concern** The abstract does not specify how diabetes was induced, the duration of treatment, the number of animals per group, or the route of administration. The toxicological group is mentioned but no outcomes are reported.

- **Resolution test** Include a full methods description with animal model, induction protocol, sample sizes, treatment duration, and toxicological endpoints, and report the corresponding results.

- **Concern ID** R1-M4  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Technical validity  
- **Claim pointer** The abstract states that molecular docking and molecular dynamics stimulation of caffeine and theobromine with IRS-1, p53, GLUT4, PI3K, and BCL2 revealed strong binding affinity as evident by RMSD and RMSF values.
