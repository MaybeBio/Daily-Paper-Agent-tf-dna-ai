### Major Concerns

- **Concern ID** R1-M1  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Evidence sufficiency  
- **Claim pointer** "experimental outcomes show that the Ni(II) complex exhibited higher binding propensity towards DNA as compared to ligand"  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, DNA binding section; location not provided  
- **Concern** The claim of higher DNA binding propensity for the complex over the ligand is stated without any quantitative data. No binding constants, comparison metrics, or assay conditions are provided.  
- **Why it matters** This is a central biophysical claim of the study. Without numerical values (e.g., Kb, ΔG) and appropriate statistical treatment, the claim cannot be evaluated or reproduced.  
- **Resolution test** Provide binding constants (e.g., Kb values) with standard errors, specify the assay method (e.g., UV-vis titration, fluorescence, viscosity), and include a comparison table for ligand versus complex under identical conditions.

- **Concern ID** R1-M2  
- **Severity** Major  
- **Blocking** Yes  
- **Axis** Technical soundness  
- **Claim pointer** "various quantum chemical parameters were calculated" and "electronic and related reactivity behaviors of the ligand and five Ni(II) complexes were studied using B3LYP/6-31 + + G**/LANL2DZ level"  
- **Evidence pointer** Abstract, computational section; location not provided  
- **Concern** The DFT methodology is incompletely specified. The basis set combination (6-31++G** for light atoms, LANL2DZ for nickel) is stated, but no details on solvation model, dispersion corrections, convergence criteria, or validation against experimental data are given.  
- **Why it matters** DFT results are only meaningful if the computational protocol is fully described and benchmarked. Without this, the calculated parameters (e.g., HOMO-LUMO gaps, reactivity descriptors) cannot be assessed for reliability.  
- **Resolution test** Provide full computational details including software version, functional verification, solvation treatment, and a comparison of calculated geometric or spectroscopic parameters with experimental values (e.g., X-ray bond lengths, IR frequencies).
