Regardless to overwhelming quantum of cancer research worldwide, there are few drugs on the market to treat disease conditions. This is owing to multiple process inferences of drug targets in integrated pathways for invasion, growth, and metastasis. Over the past years, the death rate due to breast cancer has been increasing, that set the stage for improved better treatment. Therefore, there is a persistent and vital demand for innovative development of drugs to treat breast cancer. Many studies have reported that more than 60% of breast cancers are Estrogen receptor-alpha (ERalpha)-positive tumours and a key transcription factor, Estrogen receptor-alpha (ERalpha) was believed to promote proliferation of breast cancer cells. In this study, 150 ns of molecular dynamics was performed for protein-ligand complex to retrieve the potential stable conformations. The most populated dynamics cluster of 4-Hydroxytamoxifen intact with active site amino acid was selected to generate dynamacophore model (dynamic pharmacophore). Further, internal model validation with AU-ROC values  approximately 0.93 indicate the best model to screen library. The refined hits are funnelled in pharmacokinetics/dynamics, CDOCKER molecular docking, MM-GBSA and density functional theory to identify the promising ERalpha ligand candidates.Communicated by Ramaswamy H.
