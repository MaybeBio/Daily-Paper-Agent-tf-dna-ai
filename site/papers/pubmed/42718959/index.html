In silico Identification of Key Genes and Repurposable Drugs for Triple-Negative Breast Cancer.

Zarin, Nazia; Tanha, Tasnim Hosen; Nishad, Mimuna; Parvin, Rehana; Ashad, Redwan; Islam, Md Sajedul; Haque, Shaila
Bioinformatics and biology insights · 2026-09-10 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过转录组和网络分析识别三阴性乳腺癌关键基因，并利用分子对接和MD模拟筛选药物，但核心是药物-蛋白互作而非TF-DNA结合，仅涉及转录因子调控网络分析，与课题聚焦的TF-DNA互作机制建模关联较弱。

摘要

Triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) is a highly aggressive and treatment-resistant subtype, lacking HER2, progesterone, and estrogen receptors. Systemic chemotherapy remains the primary treatment due to the absence of molecular targets, often leading to poor prognosis and high recurrence. This study used an integrated in silico transcriptomic and network-based bioinformatics approach to identify TNBC key genes (TKGs) and explore potential therapeutic candidates. Four TNBC microarray datasets (GSE36295, GSE38959, GSE45827, and GSE65194) were analyzed with the LIMMA algorithm in GEO2R, revealing 315 TNBC-shared differentially expressed genes (TSDEGs). Eight strongly connected TKGs were identified via topological analysis and protein-protein interaction (PPI) network construction across the STRING and IMEx databases from TSDEGs: CDK1, TOP2A, CCNB1, CDK2, FN1, UBC, PRKDC, and PARP1. Enrichment analysis of biological processes, molecular functions, cellular components, and KEGG pathways, combined with regulatory network analysis involving transcription factors and microRNAs, identified pathogenic roles of these genes. CDK1 and CDK2 were identified as the top pharmacological targets for molecular docking studies. Finally, TKG-guided top-ranked two drug molecules (Alsterpaullone and BLU-222) emerged as promising repurposed therapeutic candidates for TNBC. The absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity (ADMET) and drug-likeness analyses showed these molecules have favorable pharmacokinetic properties. Molecular dynamics simulations over 100 nanoseconds demonstrated stable binding interactions and favorable behavior for the complexes CDK1-Alsterpaullone and CDK2-BLU-222, as indicated by root mean square deviation, fluctuation, and molecular interactions generalized Born surface area. Overall, these findings may aid in diagnosing and treating TNBC, providing valuable resources for future therapeutic strategies.

摘要翻译

三阴性乳腺癌（TNBC）是一种高度侵袭性且难治的亚型，缺乏HER2、孕激素和雌激素受体。由于缺乏分子靶点，系统性化疗仍是主要治疗手段，但常导致预后不良和高复发率。本研究采用整合的计算机模拟转录组学和基于网络的生物信息学方法，以识别TNBC关键基因（TKGs）并探索潜在的治疗候选药物。通过GEO2R中的LIMMA算法分析了四个TNBC微阵列数据集（GSE36295、GSE38959、GSE45827和GSE65194），共揭示315个TNBC共享差异表达基因（TSDEGs）。通过跨STRING和IMEx数据库的拓扑分析及蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用（PPI）网络构建，从TSDEGs中鉴定出八个强连接的关键基因：CDK1、TOP2A、CCNB1、CDK2、FN1、UBC、PRKDC和PARP1。对生物学过程、分子功能、细胞组分及KEGG通路的富集分析，结合涉及转录因子和microRNA的调控网络分析，确定了这些基因的致病作用。CDK1和CDK2被确定为分子对接研究的首要药理学靶点。最终，基于TKG引导的排名前两位的药物分子（Alsterpaullone和BLU-222）成为TNBC有前景的药物重定位治疗候选物。吸收、分布、代谢、排泄和毒性（ADMET）及药物相似性分析显示，这些分子具有良好的药代动力学特性。超过100纳秒的分子动力学模拟表明，CDK1-Alsterpaullone和CDK2-BLU-222复合物具有稳定的结合相互作用和有利行为，这由均方根偏差、波动及广义Born表面积分子相互作用所指示。总体而言，这些发现可能有助于TNBC的诊断和治疗，为未来的治疗策略提供宝贵资源。
