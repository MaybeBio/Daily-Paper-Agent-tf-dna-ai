Study on the Antidepressant Effect and Mechanism of Water Extract of Semiliquidambar cathayensis (WESC) Based on the GSK3beta/NF-kappaB Signaling Pathway.

Yang, Li; He, Junhui; Li, Dongmei; Li, Yi; Su, Qibiao; Liang, Hongning; Yuan, Jiantong; Lai, Kedao; Wei, Guining
Chemistry & biodiversity · 2026-09-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究半枫荷水提物抗抑郁作用，涉及分子对接但对象为小分子-蛋白，非蛋白质-DNA互作，且无AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题关联弱。

摘要

Water extract of Semiliquidambar cathayensis (WESC) exhibits favorable therapeutic effects against depression, yet its pharmacodynamic material basis and mechanism of action remain unclear. In this study, ultra-high-performance liquid chromatography coupled with quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometry (UPLC-Q-TOF-MS) was employed to analyze the chemical constituents of WESC. Network pharmacology and molecular docking were further applied to screen the antidepressant mechanism basis and potential targets of WESC. Lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced mouse and PC12 cell models were established as in vivo depressive animal model and in vitro cellular injury model, respectively. Combined with pathological examination and molecular biological techniques, the antidepressant mechanism of WESC was systematically investigated. Results showed that a total of 14 chemical components were identified from WESC. Network pharmacology and molecular docking analyses indicated that GSK3beta was one of the key targets, and the active components in WESC possessed strong binding affinities with GSK3beta, Axin1, and NF-kappaB. Pathological studies demonstrated that WESC significantly alleviated inflammatory infiltration in the mouse prefrontal cortex and hippocampal neuronal damage. Molecular biological analyses revealed that WESC markedly downregulated the expression of proteins related to the GSK3beta/NF-kappaB signaling pathway in LPS-stimulated mice and PC12 cells, thereby attenuating neuroinflammation, promoting neural plasticity, and exerting antidepressant effects.

摘要翻译

半枫荷水提取物（WESC）对抑郁症表现出良好的治疗效果，但其药效物质基础及作用机制尚不明确。本研究采用超高效液相色谱-四极杆飞行时间质谱（UPLC-Q-TOF-MS）分析WESC的化学成分。进一步应用网络药理学和分子对接技术筛选WESC的抗抑郁机制基础及潜在靶点。分别建立脂多糖（LPS）诱导的小鼠和PC12细胞模型，作为体内抑郁动物模型和体外细胞损伤模型。结合病理学检查及分子生物学技术，系统研究WESC的抗抑郁机制。结果表明，从WESC中共鉴定出14种化学成分。网络药理学和分子对接分析显示，GSK3beta是关键靶点之一，WESC中的活性成分与GSK3beta、Axin1及NF-kappaB具有较强的结合亲和力。病理学研究证实，WESC显著减轻小鼠前额叶皮层的炎症浸润及海马神经元损伤。分子生物学分析揭示，WESC显著下调LPS刺激的小鼠和PC12细胞中GSK3beta/NF-kappaB信号通路相关蛋白的表达，从而减轻神经炎症、促进神经可塑性，并发挥抗抑郁作用。
