ZUP1 contributes to cisplatin resistance and treg-related signaling in non-small cell lung cancer.

Xiong, Wanyan; Deng, Zhixuan; Ding, Fang; Xiao, Zhike; Shi, Wang; Gu, Gang; Yan, Nan
Scientific reports · 2026-09-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究ZUP1在非小细胞肺癌顺铂耐药中的作用及Treg相关信号，涉及分子对接但核心是耐药机制和免疫微环境，非蛋白质-DNA互作或TF-DNA结合机制，与本课题无关。

摘要

Acquired cisplatin resistance limits platinum chemotherapy efficacy in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and is accompanied by tumor immune microenvironment (TME) remodeling. We integrated public NSCLC single-cell RNA-seq datasets to compare cellular composition and CellChat-inferred communication between post-chemotherapy remission and relapse/resistant tumors, and validated ZUP1 expression and prognostic relevance in TCGA and external cohorts. In cisplatin-resistant A549/R and SK-MES-1/R cells, we generated stable ZUP1 knockdown and assessed proliferation, tumorsphere growth and formation, apoptosis (Annexin V/PI) and BAX/BCL2 expression, and xenograft growth. Tumor cell-peripheral Treg co-cultures were used to quantify the proportion of FOXP3(+) cells within the gated CD4(+) population. Natural-product screening was guided by molecular docking, and ZUP1-compound binding was evaluated by surface plasmon resonance (SPR). Peimisine responses were compared between parental and cisplatin-resistant cells. In A549/R cells, experimentally measured viability across a peimisine-cisplatin concentration matrix and cisplatin dose-response curves at fixed peimisine concentrations were used to evaluate changes in cisplatin sensitivity. Resistant tumors exhibited remodeled cellular composition and enhanced intercellular communication, including increased epithelial-to-Treg signaling. ZUP1 was upregulated in resistant models and associated with unfavorable prognosis. ZUP1 knockdown suppressed growth and tumorsphere formation, increased apoptosis, reduced tumorigenicity in vivo, and reduced the proportion of CD4(+)FOXP3(+) Treg-like cells in co-culture. Peimisine directly bound ZUP1 by SPR, inhibited resistant cell growth, and was associated with lower estimated cisplatin IC(5)(0) values in A549/R cells at fixed peimisine concentrations. ZUP1 contributes to the maintenance of cisplatin-resistant growth and is associated with enhanced Treg-related immunosuppressive signaling in NSCLC. Peimisine directly binds ZUP1 and may serve as a preliminary natural-product lead for further target-validation and combination studies.

摘要翻译

获得性顺铂耐药限制了非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）中铂类化疗的疗效，并伴随肿瘤免疫微环境（TME）重塑。我们整合了公开的NSCLC单细胞RNA-seq数据集，比较化疗后缓解与复发/耐药肿瘤之间的细胞组成和CellChat推断的通讯，并在TCGA和外部队列中验证了ZUP1表达及预后相关性。在顺铂耐药的A549/R和SK-MES-1/R细胞中，我们生成了稳定的ZUP1敲低，并评估了增殖、肿瘤球生长和形成、凋亡（Annexin V/PI）及BAX/BCL2表达，以及异种移植瘤生长。使用肿瘤细胞-外周Treg共培养来量化门控CD4(+)群体中FOXP3(+)细胞的比例。天然产物筛选以分子对接为指导，并通过表面等离子体共振（SPR）评估ZUP1-化合物结合。比较了亲本和顺铂耐药细胞对贝母辛（Peimisine）的反应。在A549/R细胞中，通过实验测量的贝母辛-顺铂浓度矩阵下的活力，以及固定贝母辛浓度下的顺铂剂量反应曲线，用于评估顺铂敏感性的变化。耐药肿瘤表现出重塑的细胞组成和增强的细胞间通讯，包括增加的上皮细胞到Treg信号传导。ZUP1在耐药模型中上调，并与不良预后相关。ZUP1敲低抑制了生长和肿瘤球形成，增加了凋亡，降低了体内致瘤性，并减少了共培养中CD4(+)FOXP3(+) Treg样细胞的比例。贝母辛通过SPR直接结合ZUP1，抑制耐药细胞生长，并在固定贝母辛浓度下与A549/R细胞中估计的顺铂IC(5)(0)值降低相关。ZUP1有助于维持顺铂耐药生长，并与NSCLC中增强的Treg相关免疫抑制信号传导相关。贝母辛直接结合ZUP1，可能作为初步的天然产物先导化合物，用于进一步靶点验证和联合研究。
