Development and validation of a machine learning clinicogenomic model to improve prognostic stratification in ER-positive/HER2-negative early breast cancer.

Sarafidis, Michail; Sifakis, Emmanouil G; Bergh, Jonas; Foukakis, Theodoros; Matikas, Alexios
Breast cancer research : BCR · 2026-09-17 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文开发机器学习临床基因组模型用于乳腺癌预后分层，不涉及蛋白质-DNA互作或TF-DNA结合机制，与本课题无关。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Several gene expression signatures (GESs) are used for risk stratification in estrogen receptor-positive/human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative (ER-positive/HER2-negative) early breast cancer. Recent integrative approaches combine tumour proliferation, estrogen receptor signalling, immune activity, and clinicopathologic features, yielding gains in prognostic accuracy. We therefore developed a machine-learning-based clinicogenomic prognostic model integrating a research-grade implementation of an established GES with immune-related and clinicopathologic features to improve prognostic stratification in ER-positive/HER2-negative early breast cancer. METHODS: This retrospective integrative analysis of publicly available and institutional microarray and RNA-sequencing data included four independent datasets of systemically untreated or endocrine-treated patients (n = 5132). One dataset was used for the development of a random survival forest (RSF) model, and three datasets were used for external validation. The RSF model incorporated a research-grade implementation of the 21-gene recurrence score (RS), the 14-gene immunoglobulin signature, and clinicopathologic features. Model performance was evaluated relative to the standalone research-grade implementation of the 21-gene RS using measures of risk stratification, discrimination, and prediction error. Feature contributions were examined using time-dependent explainability analyses. RESULTS: In external validation, the RSF model identified larger low-risk groups than the standalone 21-gene RS, with relative increases of 33.8 to 111.8%, while maintaining similar or higher horizon-specific survival estimates. The RSF model also improved discrimination over the standalone 21-gene RS across validation datasets, with absolute increases of 0.035 to 0.058 in the concordance index and 0.030 to 0.064 in the integrated area under the time-dependent receiver operating characteristic curve. Risk separation, measured by the difference in restricted mean survival time, was consistently greater for the RSF model, whereas prediction error was comparable or lower according to Cox-recalibrated integrated Brier score. Explainability analyses indicated time-dependent feature contributions, with the 21-gene RS and clinicopathologic variables driving early prognostic performance and the immune component contributing a smaller but more stable effect over time. CONCLUSIONS: This explainable clinicogenomic machine learning model integrates research-grade molecular, immune-related, and clinicopathologic features and improves prognostic stratification in retrospective ER-positive/HER2-negative early breast cancer datasets. These findings support its potential as a more informative and biologically grounded approach to early breast cancer risk assessment.

摘要翻译

背景：在雌激素受体阳性/人表皮生长因子受体2阴性（ER阳性/HER2阴性）早期乳腺癌中，多种基因表达特征（GESs）被用于风险分层。近年来的整合方法结合了肿瘤增殖、雌激素受体信号、免疫活性及临床病理特征，从而提高了预后准确性。因此，我们开发了一种基于机器学习的临床基因组预后模型，将已确立的GES的研究级实现与免疫相关及临床病理特征相结合，以改善ER阳性/HER2阴性早期乳腺癌的预后分层。方法：这项回顾性整合分析使用了公开可用的及机构内部的微阵列和RNA测序数据，包括四个独立数据集，涉及未经系统治疗或接受内分泌治疗的患者（n = 5132）。其中一个数据集用于开发随机生存森林（RSF）模型，另外三个数据集用于外部验证。RSF模型纳入了21基因复发评分（RS）的研究级实现、14基因免疫球蛋白特征及临床病理特征。模型性能相对于独立的研究级21基因RS实现进行评估，使用风险分层、区分度和预测误差等指标。特征贡献通过时间依赖性可解释性分析进行检验。结果：在外部验证中，RSF模型识别出的低风险组比独立的21基因RS更大，相对增加33.8%至111.8%，同时保持相似或更高的时间点特定生存估计。RSF模型在验证数据集上的区分度也优于独立的21基因RS，一致性指数绝对增加0.035至0.058，时间依赖性受试者工作特征曲线下积分面积绝对增加0.030至0.064。通过限制平均生存时间差异测量的风险分离在RSF模型中始终更大，而根据Cox重校准的积分Brier评分，预测误差相当或更低。可解释性分析表明特征贡献具有时间依赖性，21基因RS和临床病理变量驱动早期预后性能，而免疫组分随时间贡献较小但更稳定。结论：这种可解释的临床基因组机器学习模型整合了研究级分子、免疫相关及临床病理特征，并在回顾性ER阳性/HER2阴性早期乳腺癌数据集中改善了预后分层。这些发现支持其作为早期乳腺癌风险评估中更具信息性和生物学基础的方法的潜力。
