Xiaobanxia decoction, a compound Chinese herbal medicine, attenuates chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting via restoring AMPK/TFEB-mediated BNIP3/NIX mitophagy.

Feng, Lei; Zhai, Ya-Rong; Liang, Wan; Kan, Shao-Jun; Nie, Ke
Journal of integrative medicine · 2026-09-09 · 仅摘要 · 原文

研究中药复方通过AMPK/TFEB通路调控线粒体自噬缓解化疗呕吐，不涉及蛋白质-DNA互作或TF-DNA结合机制，且方法以实验为主，无AI/物理模拟建模。

摘要

OBJECTIVE: Xiaobanxia decoction (XBXD), a classical Chinese antiemetic formula, has potential benefits for treating chemotherapy-induced nausea and vomiting (CINV). The present study investigates its antiemetic effect and explores the possible mechanisms. METHODS: Network pharmacology and molecular docking were used to predict potential therapeutic targets and pathways. The efficacy and mechanism of XBXD were evaluated using a cisplatin-induced rat pica model and an intestinal epithelial cell line-6 (IEC-6) cell injury model. Antiemetic effects were assessed by measuring kaolin intake, food consumption, and body weight. Gastric antrum and ileum pathological changes were examined by hematoxylin and eosin staining. Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assays were used to assess tissue levels of interleukin-1beta (IL-1beta), interleukin-18 (IL-18), and reactive oxygen species (ROS). Mitochondrial injury and mitochondrial ROS were evaluated using transmission electron microscopy and fluorescence microscopy. Mitophagy and adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase (AMPK)/transcription factor EB (TFEB) pathway activation were assessed using Western blotting and immunofluorescence. RESULTS: Compared with the model group, XBXD significantly reduced kaolin consumption, alleviated gastric antrum and ileum pathological damage, and decreased IL-1beta, IL-18 and ROS levels in both gastric antrum and ileum tissues. XBXD also improved mitochondrial ultrastructural morphology, reduced mitochondrial ROS, and restored mitochondrial membrane potential in vivo and in vitro. Furthermore, XBXD increased the microtubule-associated protein 1 light chain 3-II/-I (LC3-II/LC3-I) ratio, reduced sequestosome-1 (p62) accumulation, and upregulated BCL2/adenovirus E1B 19 kDa interacting protein 3 (BNIP3) and Nip3-like protein X (NIX) expression, indicating restoration of mitophagy. Mechanistically, the AMPK/TFEB signaling pathway was triggered by the XBXD treatment. CONCLUSION: XBXD ameliorates CINV in association with activation of the AMPK/TFEB pathway and the restoration of BNIP3/NIX-mediated mitophagy. 摘要翻译

目的：小半夏汤（XBXD）是一种经典的中医止吐方剂，对治疗化疗引起的恶心呕吐（CINV）具有潜在益处。本研究探讨其止吐作用并探索可能的机制。方法：采用网络药理学和分子对接预测潜在的治疗靶点和通路。通过顺铂诱导的大鼠异食癖模型和肠上皮细胞系-6（IEC-6）细胞损伤模型评估XBXD的疗效和机制。通过测量高岭土摄入量、食物消耗量和体重来评估止吐效果。采用苏木精-伊红染色检查胃窦和回肠的病理变化。使用酶联免疫吸附试验评估组织中白细胞介素-1β（IL-1β）、白细胞介素-18（IL-18）和活性氧（ROS）的水平。使用透射电子显微镜和荧光显微镜评估线粒体损伤和线粒体ROS。使用Western blotting和免疫荧光评估线粒体自噬和腺苷酸活化蛋白激酶（AMPK）/转录因子EB（TFEB）通路的激活。结果：与模型组相比，XBXD显著减少高岭土消耗，减轻胃窦和回肠的病理损伤，并降低胃窦和回肠组织中IL-1β、IL-18和ROS的水平。XBXD还改善了线粒体超微结构形态，减少了线粒体ROS，并在体内和体外恢复了线粒体膜电位。此外，XBXD增加了微管相关蛋白1轻链3-II/-I（LC3-II/LC3-I）的比值，减少了sequestosome-1（p62）的积累，并上调了BCL2/腺病毒E1B 19 kDa相互作用蛋白3（BNIP3）和Nip3样蛋白X（NIX）的表达，表明线粒体自噬得到恢复。机制上，XBXD处理触发了AMPK/TFEB信号通路。结论：XBXD通过激活AMPK/TFEB通路和恢复BNIP3/NIX介导的线粒体自噬来改善CINV。请引用本文为：Feng L, Zhai YR, Liang W, Kan SJ, Nie K.
