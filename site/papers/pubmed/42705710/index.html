An antioxidant and hepatoprotective peptide derived from peony seed meal: Dual mechanisms through activation of the Keap1/Nrf2/HO-1 pathway and inhibition of mitochondrial apoptosis.

Wang, Bing; Fei, Bo; Yan, Xueshan; Xiao, Zhenxiang; Dong, Yingying; Jiang, Ting; Shao, Shuai; Yan, Mingming
Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) · 2026-09-07 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究牡丹籽粕抗氧化肽AT-8通过Keap1/Nrf2/HO-1通路保护肝细胞，属于纯蛋白-小分子肽功能研究，无蛋白质-DNA互作、无AI或物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

Peony seed meal (PSM), a byproduct of peony seed oil processing, is rich in protein but is currently used primarily as animal feed in the livestock industry, leaving its protein resources largely underutilized. Herein, PSM was used as the raw material, and a combination of ultrasound-assisted enzymatic hydrolysis and ultrafiltration was employed to prepare a naturally active peptide fraction (PSMPH) from PSM protein (PSMP). Compared with PSMP, PSMPH demonstrated superior structural characteristics and antioxidant activity. Using liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry, bioactivity prediction, and molecular docking analyses, the peptide AT-8 (APGGYGGT) was identified as a potential antioxidant candidate from PSMPH and subsequently synthesized using solid-phase synthesis. Safety evaluation further confirmed that AT-8 produced no cytotoxic side effects in five normal somatic cell lines. In H(2)O(2)-induced oxidative damage in LO2 hepatocytes, AT-8 significantly reduced alanine aminotransferase, aspartate aminotransferase, and malondialdehyde levels while upregulating the activities of superoxide dismutase, catalase, and glutathione peroxidase compared with PSMPH. Pathway-specific validation further demonstrated that AT-8 exerts hepatoprotective effects through a cascade mechanism involving Nrf2-HO-1 activation, enhanced antioxidant defense, and indirect inhibition of Bax/caspase-3. This study identified AT-8 as an antioxidant hepatoprotective peptide derived from PSM and systematically elucidated its molecular mechanism of action, thereby providing a scientific basis for the high-value utilization of PSM.

摘要翻译

牡丹籽粕（PSM）是牡丹籽油加工的副产物，富含蛋白质，但目前主要用作畜牧业的动物饲料，其蛋白质资源未得到充分利用。本研究以PSM为原料，采用超声辅助酶解与超滤相结合的方法，从PSM蛋白（PSMP）中制备了天然活性肽组分（PSMPH）。与PSMP相比，PSMPH表现出更优的结构特性和抗氧化活性。通过液相色谱-串联质谱、生物活性预测和分子对接分析，从PSMPH中鉴定出肽AT-8（APGGYGGT）作为潜在的抗氧化候选物，并随后采用固相合成法进行合成。安全性评价进一步证实，AT-8在五种正常体细胞系中未产生细胞毒性副作用。在H(2)O(2)诱导的LO2肝细胞氧化损伤中，与PSMPH相比，AT-8显著降低了丙氨酸氨基转移酶、天冬氨酸氨基转移酶和丙二醛水平，同时上调了超氧化物歧化酶、过氧化氢酶和谷胱甘肽过氧化物酶的活性。通路特异性验证进一步表明，AT-8通过涉及Nrf2-HO-1激活、增强抗氧化防御以及间接抑制Bax/caspase-3的级联机制发挥肝保护作用。本研究鉴定出AT-8为源自PSM的抗氧化肝保护肽，并系统阐明了其分子作用机制，从而为PSM的高值化利用提供了科学依据。
