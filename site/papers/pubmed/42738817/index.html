Male and Female Mice Show Similar Fear Memory Performance Despite Hippocampal Immediate Early Gene Expression Differences During Encoding and Consolidation.

McDonald, Katherine O; Yu, Temmie; Prabhu, Aditi; Aton, Sara J
Cells · 2026-09-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究比较雄雌小鼠情境恐惧记忆中的海马即刻早期基因表达差异，属于神经行为学与基因表达分析，不涉及蛋白质-DNA互作、结合建模或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

Accurate and efficient memory processing is essential for survival. A body of ongoing work in both human subjects and animal models suggests that memory processing may differ substantially between males and females. In mice, contextual fear memory (CFM) encoding, consolidation, and recall have been well studied, and the mouse hippocampus and amygdala have been implicated in these processes. The present pilot study addresses whether the activation of these brain regions differs substantially between male and female mice at each stage of CFM processing. We find that male and female mice show no differences in sleep behavior, which is essential for CFM consolidation, following single-trial contextual fear conditioning (CFC). We also find no significant differences in CFM recall performance between male and female mice. However, females show a trend for larger increases in CA1 cFos expression, relative to males, during CFM encoding. On the other hand, only males-but not females-show an apparent increase in cFos expression among dentate gyrus (DG) granule cells during CFM consolidation. Males also show a trend for a larger apparent reduction in cFos in CA1 and CA3 during CFM consolidation, relative to females. These preliminary findings highlight the idea that the neurobiological underpinnings of memory processing may differ between males and females, even when performance during recall is identical.

摘要翻译

准确且高效的记忆处理对生存至关重要。在人类受试者和动物模型中进行的持续研究表明，记忆处理在雄性和雌性之间可能存在显著差异。在小鼠中，情境恐惧记忆（CFM）的编码、巩固和回忆已被广泛研究，并且小鼠海马体和杏仁核被认为参与这些过程。本初步研究探讨了在CFM处理的每个阶段，这些脑区的激活在雄性和雌性小鼠之间是否存在显著差异。我们发现，在单次试验情境恐惧条件化（CFC）后，雄性和雌性小鼠在睡眠行为上没有差异，而睡眠对CFM巩固至关重要。我们还发现，雄性和雌性小鼠在CFM回忆表现上没有显著差异。然而，在CFM编码期间，雌性小鼠的CA1区cFos表达增加趋势相对于雄性更大。另一方面，仅在雄性而非雌性小鼠中，在CFM巩固期间，齿状回（DG）颗粒细胞中的cFos表达表现出明显增加。此外，在CFM巩固期间，雄性小鼠的CA1和CA3区cFos表达减少趋势相对于雌性更大。这些初步发现强调了记忆处理的神经生物学基础在雄性和雌性之间可能不同，即使在回忆表现相同的情况下也是如此。
