Electronic structure, DNA-binding, and biological activity correlations in transition metal complexes of a tetradentate Schiff base: experimental and computational studies.

Michael, Samuel; Jeyaraman, Porkodi; Gnanamani, Lavanya; Raman, Natarajan; Tamilselvan, Silambarasan; Nagaraj, Karuppiah
Journal of biomolecular structure & dynamics · 2026-09-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究过渡金属-Schiff碱配合物与小牛胸腺DNA的结合，使用光谱、电化学、DFT和分子对接，但对象为小分子金属配合物而非蛋白质-DNA互作，且无AI或MD模拟，仅部分方法可借鉴。

摘要

A novel tetradentate Schiff base ligand (L), synthesized by the condensation of 2,6-diaminopyridine and 3,5-dichlorosalicylaldehyde, and its Cu(II), Ni(II), Co(II), Zn(II), and VO(IV) complexes were prepared and characterized by elemental analysis, FT-IR, UV-Vis, NMR, ESI-mass, magnetic susceptibility, thermal, and EPR studies. Spectroscopic evidence indicates coordination of the ligand through an N(2)O(2) donor set. Based on the available spectroscopic, magnetic, and analytical data, the Cu(II), Ni(II), and Co(II) complexes are proposed to adopt predominantly square-planar geometries, while the Zn(II) and VO(IV) complexes are proposed to possess distorted tetrahedral and square-pyramidal geometries, respectively. DNA-binding interactions with calf thymus DNA were investigated using UV-Vis absorption, viscosity, and electrochemical techniques. The observed hypochromism, minor bathochromic shifts, and viscosity changes suggest a predominantly intercalative binding mode, with intrinsic binding constants (K(b)) ranging from 3.59 x 10(4) to 6.43 x 10(4) M(-1); the Cu(II) complex exhibited the highest affinity. Electrochemical studies further supported complex-DNA interactions. Density functional theory and molecular electrostatic potential analyses revealed enhanced electronic stabilization, with the Cu(II) and VO(IV) complexes exhibiting the smallest HOMO-LUMO energy gaps. These findings are consistent with their stronger DNA-binding affinity and higher biological activity. Molecular docking provided supportive evidence for favorable biomolecular interactions, particularly for the Cu(II) complex. Metal coordination enhanced the antibacterial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory activities compared with the free ligand, with the Cu(II) and VO(IV) complexes showing the most promising overall performance. The results demonstrate correlations between electronic structure, DNA-binding behavior, and biological activity in this class of transition metal complexes.

摘要翻译

通过2,6-二氨基吡啶与3,5-二氯水杨醛缩合合成了一种新型四齿席夫碱配体（L），并制备了其Cu(II)、Ni(II)、Co(II)、Zn(II)和VO(IV)配合物，通过元素分析、FT-IR、UV-Vis、NMR、ESI-质谱、磁化率、热分析和EPR研究对其进行了表征。光谱证据表明，配体通过N(2)O(2)供体集进行配位。基于现有的光谱、磁性和分析数据，Cu(II)、Ni(II)和Co(II)配合物被认为主要采用平面正方形几何构型，而Zn(II)和VO(IV)配合物分别被认为具有畸变四面体和四方锥几何构型。使用UV-Vis吸收、粘度和电化学技术研究了与小牛胸腺DNA的DNA结合相互作用。观察到的减色效应、轻微红移和粘度变化表明主要为嵌入结合模式，固有结合常数（K(b)）范围为3.59 x 10(4)至6.43 x 10(4) M(-1)；其中Cu(II)配合物表现出最高的亲和力。电化学研究进一步支持了配合物-DNA相互作用。密度泛函理论和分子静电势分析揭示了增强的电子稳定性，其中Cu(II)和VO(IV)配合物表现出最小的HOMO-LUMO能隙。这些发现与其更强的DNA结合亲和力和更高的生物活性一致。分子对接为有利的生物分子相互作用提供了支持性证据，尤其是对Cu(II)配合物。与自由配体相比，金属配位增强了抗菌、抗氧化和抗炎活性，其中Cu(II)和VO(IV)配合物表现出最有前景的综合性能。结果表明，在这类过渡金属配合物中，电子结构、DNA结合行为和生物活性之间存在相关性。
