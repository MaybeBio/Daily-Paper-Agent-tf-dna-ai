Binding analysis of the response regulator NarL protein to the promoter of the O(6)-methylguanine-DNA methyltransferase (ogt) gene in Salmonella Typhimurium.

Sahoo, Pravas Ranjan; Kumar, Ajay; Pashupathi, M; Sahoo, Jyochhna Rani
Molecular biology reports · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过EMSA、ITC和分子对接研究NarL转录因子与ogt启动子的结合，属TF-DNA互作体系，但方法以实验为主、分子对接较简单，AI/物理模拟深度不足。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Salmonella Typhimurium (STM) is a gram-negative bacterium that causes severe gastrointestinal disorders in both animals and humans. The regulation of DNA repair genes is critical for maintaining genomic stability of the bacteria. O(6)-methylguanine DNA methyltransferase (Ogt), plays a vital role in repairing alkylated DNA in STM; however, the transcriptional regulation of ogt gene remains poorly characterized. Furthermore, NarL is a transcriptional regulator, involved in the pathogenesis of STM under anaerobic condition. Therefore, this study investigated the interaction between NarL protein and the promoter region of the ogt gene. METHODS: In this study, narl gene was cloned in pET32a vector and NarL protein was expressed in Escherichia coli BL21 (DE3). Subsequently, the ogt gene promoter (pogt) was selected, amplified, cloned and its activity was evaluated. Electrophoretic mobility shift assay (EMSA), isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC), molecular docking were employed to elucidate the interaction between NarL protein and ogt promoter. Furthermore, the regulatory role of NarL in ogt gene expression was validated in vivo using RT-qPCR and beta-galactosidase assay. RESULTS: This study resulted that NarL protein interacts specifically with the ogt promoter, as confirmed by EMSA and ITC, with DeltaG of - 9.42 kcal mol(-)(1). Furthermore, RT-qPCR and beta-galactosidase assays demonstrated that deletion of narl significantly (P </= 0.01) decreased ogt transcript levels and promoter activity than wild Salmonella Typhimurium, whereas exogenous supplementation of recombinant NarL protein restored the expression. These findings suggest that NarL plays a potential regulatory role in ogt gene expression in response to environmental signals. CONCLUSION: These findings highlight an interaction between NarL protein and the promoter region of ogt gene in Salmonella Typhimurium, linking nitrogen metabolism with the DNA repair pathway in STM, which may contribute to the bacterial survival under nitrosative stress.

摘要翻译

背景：鼠伤寒沙门氏菌（Salmonella Typhimurium, STM）是一种革兰氏阴性细菌，可引起动物和人类的严重胃肠道疾病。DNA修复基因的调控对于维持细菌的基因组稳定性至关重要。O(6)-甲基鸟嘌呤DNA甲基转移酶（Ogt）在STM中修复烷基化DNA中发挥关键作用；然而，ogt基因的转录调控机制仍鲜有表征。此外，NarL是一种转录调控因子，参与STM在厌氧条件下的致病性。因此，本研究探讨了NarL蛋白与ogt基因启动子区域之间的相互作用。方法：在本研究中，将narl基因克隆至pET32a载体中，并在大肠杆菌BL21 (DE3)中表达NarL蛋白。随后，选择、扩增并克隆ogt基因启动子（pogt），并评估其活性。采用电泳迁移率变动分析（EMSA）、等温滴定量热法（ITC）和分子对接来阐明NarL蛋白与ogt启动子之间的相互作用。此外，通过RT-qPCR和β-半乳糖苷酶测定法在体内验证了NarL在ogt基因表达中的调控作用。结果：本研究结果表明，NarL蛋白与ogt启动子特异性相互作用，经EMSA和ITC确认，ΔG为-9.42 kcal mol⁻¹。此外，RT-qPCR和β-半乳糖苷酶测定表明，与野生型鼠伤寒沙门氏菌相比，narl缺失显著（P ≤ 0.01）降低了ogt转录水平和启动子活性，而外源补充重组NarL蛋白则恢复了表达。这些发现表明，NarL在响应环境信号时对ogt基因表达具有潜在调控作用。结论：这些发现强调了鼠伤寒沙门氏菌中NarL蛋白与ogt基因启动子区域之间的相互作用，将氮代谢与STM中的DNA修复途径联系起来，这可能有助于细菌在亚硝化应激下的生存。
