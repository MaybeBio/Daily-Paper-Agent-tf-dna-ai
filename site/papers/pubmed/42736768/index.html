Transcriptomic integration reveals immune heterogeneity in vascular dementia and identifies ELF2 as a candidate therapeutic target.

Liang, Li; Shen, Cuiqin; Huang, Shiren; Hu, Minglu; Zhu, Jian
Medicine · 2026-09-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究整合转录组与单细胞数据分析血管性痴呆的免疫异质性，仅用分子对接初步评估ELF2与药物结合，缺乏蛋白质-DNA互作体系与AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题关联微弱。

摘要

Vascular dementia (VaD) is the second most prevalent form of dementia after Alzheimer's disease and is primarily driven by chronic cerebral hypoperfusion and neurovascular dysfunction. Growing evidence indicates that immune dysregulation and neuroglial-vascular injury play critical roles in disease progression; however, the molecular regulators underlying these processes remain insufficiently characterized. This study aimed to delineate immune heterogeneity and neuroglial-vascular alterations in VaD and to identify key regulatory targets involved in disease pathogenesis. Bulk RNA sequencing data (GSE122063) and single-cell transcriptomic data (GSE282111) were retrieved from the Gene Expression Omnibus database and integratively analyzed to characterize immune infiltration patterns and cellular dysregulation. Immune profiling was performed using CIBERSORT and single-sample gene set enrichment analysis. Disease-associated cell subsets and hub genes were identified using Seurat, Scissor, and high-dimensional weighted gene co-expression network analysis (hdWGCNA). Pseudotime trajectory analysis and molecular docking were subsequently applied to investigate gene expression dynamics and potential therapeutic relevance. Bulk transcriptomic analysis revealed a pronounced pro-inflammatory shift in VaD brain tissue, characterized by increased infiltration of M1 macrophages, neutrophils, and activated dendritic cells, alongside reduced levels of M2 macrophages, resting CD4+ memory T cells, and regulatory T cells. Single-cell analysis demonstrated marked loss of oligodendrocytes, astrocytes, and endothelial cells in VaD. Scissor integration showed positive associations between VaD and oligodendrocytes, astrocytes, and endothelial cells, whereas microglia and oligodendrocyte progenitor cells were negatively associated. hdWGCNA identified 3 co-expression modules; intersection of the blue module with bulk differentially expressed genes highlighted DOCK3, ELF2, and Sin3A associated protein 25, with ELF2 uniquely co-expressed across relevant cell types. Pseudotime analysis indicated sustained downregulation of ELF2 during VaD-related cellular differentiation. Molecular docking analysis suggested strong binding affinities between ELF2 and nicergoline (-7.23 kcal/mol), nimodipine (-6.92 kcal/mol), and donepezil (-6.29 kcal/mol). This integrative transcriptomic study reveals disrupted immune balance and neuroglial-vascular cell homeostasis in vascular dementia, and prioritizes ELF2 as a candidate regulator associated with immune heterogeneity in VaD.

摘要翻译

血管性痴呆（VaD）是继阿尔茨海默病之后第二常见的痴呆类型，主要由慢性脑低灌注和神经血管功能障碍驱动。越来越多的证据表明，免疫失调和神经胶质-血管损伤在疾病进展中发挥关键作用；然而，这些过程背后的分子调控因子仍未被充分表征。本研究旨在描绘VaD中的免疫异质性和神经胶质-血管改变，并识别参与疾病发病机制的关键调控靶点。从Gene Expression Omnibus数据库获取批量RNA测序数据（GSE122063）和单细胞转录组数据（GSE282111），并进行整合分析以表征免疫浸润模式和细胞失调。使用CIBERSORT和单样本基因集富集分析进行免疫谱分析。使用Seurat、Scissor和高维加权基因共表达网络分析（hdWGCNA）识别疾病相关细胞亚群和核心基因。随后应用拟时间轨迹分析和分子对接研究基因表达动态和潜在治疗相关性。批量转录组分析揭示VaD脑组织中显著的前炎症转变，其特征为M1巨噬细胞、中性粒细胞和活化树突状细胞浸润增加，同时M2巨噬细胞、静息CD4+记忆T细胞和调节性T细胞水平降低。单细胞分析显示VaD中少突胶质细胞、星形胶质细胞和内皮细胞显著丢失。Scissor整合显示VaD与少突胶质细胞、星形胶质细胞和内皮细胞呈正相关，而小胶质细胞和少突胶质前体细胞呈负相关。hdWGCNA识别出3个共表达模块；蓝色模块与批量差异表达基因的交集突出了DOCK3、ELF2和Sin3A相关蛋白25，其中ELF2在相关细胞类型中独特共表达。拟时间分析表明ELF2在VaD相关细胞分化过程中持续下调。分子对接分析提示ELF2与尼麦角林（-7.23 kcal/mol）、尼莫地平（-6.92 kcal/mol）和多奈哌齐（-6.29 kcal/mol）之间具有强结合亲和力。这项整合转录组研究揭示了血管性痴呆中免疫平衡和神经胶质-血管细胞稳态的破坏，并将ELF2优先列为与VaD免疫异质性相关的候选调控因子。
