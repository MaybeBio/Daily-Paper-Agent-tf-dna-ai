Bioinformatics Screening and Preliminary Experimental Verification of Integrated Stress Response-Associated MYC and BAG3 in Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease.

Liu, Xueping; Wang, Lu; Tang, Ting; Qian, Hang; He, Binfeng; Xu, Zhi; Wang, Guansong; Zhang, Mingzhou
International journal of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease · 2026-09-06 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过生物信息学筛选COPD中ISR相关生物标志物MYC和BAG3，虽涉及转录因子（如SNAI2）的关联分析，但核心是表达差异和诊断模型，缺乏蛋白质-DNA互作机制、结合位点预测或三维结构建模，且方法以统计和机器学习为主，无AI/物理模拟聚焦于TF-DNA互作，与本课题关联度低。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) has high morbidity and mortality, and integrated stress response (ISR) participates in its progression. Traditional COPD diagnosis relies mainly on GOLD criteria, while ISR-based auxiliary molecular markers are still lacking. This study aimed to comprehensively analyze and identify ISR-related biomarkers in COPD, and to uncover their underlying molecular mechanisms. METHODS: COPD data were obtained from the GEO database. Biomarkers were identified and validated through a comprehensive approach, including differential expression analysis, machine learning algorithms, expression level verification, and receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve analysis. Moreover, a nomogram was applied to construct an ISR-related predictive model for COPD. Enrichment analysis, immune infiltration analysis, molecular regulatory network, and drug prediction were performed to further confirm the regulatory roles of biomarkers. Furthermore, the expression patterns of biomarkers in core cells were investigated using dataset GSE173896. Furthermore, Western blotting was employed to detect the protein expression levels of biomarkers in clinical samples. RESULTS: Overall, 2 biomarkers (MYC and BAG3) were identified as significantly associated with ISR in COPD. A predictive nomogram constructed based on MYC and BAG3 demonstrated excellent diagnostic performance, with an area under the curve (AUC) value of 0.799. The biomarkers were significantly enriched in the MAPK signaling pathway and cell cycle. Notably, biomarkers were positively correlated with effector memory CD4 T cells and eosinophils. Subsequently, 36 microRNAs (miRNAs) (like hsa-miR-4699-3p), 260 transcription factors (TFs) (like SNAI2, ZBTB43, and ZNF395) and 9 drugs (like staurosporine and N-acetyl-L-cysteine) were associated with the biomarkers. Moreover, significant changes in MYC and BAG3 expression were observed during T cell differentiation. Clinically, MYC and BAG3 were significantly upregulated in COPD tissues, with protein levels increased by 58.4% and 252.2% respectively. CONCLUSION: This study identified and preliminarily verified ISR-associated MYC and BAG3, offering novel molecular references to complement conventional COPD clinical assessment.

摘要翻译

背景：慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）具有高发病率和死亡率，整合应激反应（ISR）参与其进展。传统COPD诊断主要依赖GOLD标准，而基于ISR的辅助分子标志物仍缺乏。本研究旨在全面分析并识别COPD中与ISR相关的生物标志物，并揭示其潜在分子机制。方法：COPD数据来自GEO数据库。通过综合方法（包括差异表达分析、机器学习算法、表达水平验证和受试者工作特征（ROC）曲线分析）识别并验证生物标志物。此外，应用列线图构建COPD的ISR相关预测模型。通过富集分析、免疫浸润分析、分子调控网络和药物预测进一步确认生物标志物的调控作用。进一步利用数据集GSE173896研究生物标志物在核心细胞中的表达模式。此外，采用Western blotting检测临床样本中生物标志物的蛋白表达水平。结果：总体而言，2个生物标志物（MYC和BAG3）被识别为与COPD中ISR显著相关。基于MYC和BAG3构建的预测列线图表现出优异的诊断性能，曲线下面积（AUC）值为0.799。生物标志物显著富集于MAPK信号通路和细胞周期。值得注意的是，生物标志物与效应记忆CD4 T细胞和嗜酸性粒细胞呈正相关。随后，36个微小RNA（miRNA）（如hsa-miR-4699-3p）、260个转录因子（TF）（如SNAI2、ZBTB43和ZNF395）和9种药物（如星形孢菌素和N-乙酰-L-半胱氨酸）与生物标志物相关。此外，在T细胞分化过程中观察到MYC和BAG3表达的显著变化。临床上，MYC和BAG3在COPD组织中显著上调，蛋白水平分别增加58.4%和252.2%。结论：本研究识别并初步验证了ISR相关的MYC和BAG3，为补充传统COPD临床评估提供了新的分子参考。
