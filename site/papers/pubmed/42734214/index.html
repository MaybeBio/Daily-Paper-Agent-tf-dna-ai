Bushen Huatan Formula Ameliorates Early Chronic Low-Grade Inflammation in Obese PCOS Via the TLR4/NF-kappaB Signaling Pathway: An Integrated Network Pharmacology and Experimental Study.

Zhang, Jianing; Gu, Fangyun; Li, Yunyun; Feng, Xiaoling; Kuang, Hongying; Xu, Fang; Sun, Miao
Immunity, inflammation and disease · 2026-09-14 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

摘要

OBJECTIVE: This study used an integrated network pharmacology and experimental strategy to investigate how Bushen Huatan Formula (BHF) mitigates early-stage chronic low-grade inflammation in obese polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS). METHODS: Candidate components and targets of BHF were retrieved from the TCMSP database, and protein-protein interaction, GO and KEGG enrichment, and molecular docking analyzes were performed. Predictions were then tested in a letrozole-induced PCOS rat model, in which ovarian and adipose histopathology and TLR4/NF-kappaB expression were examined at 6 and 12 weeks; isolated granulosa cells (GCs) were further exposed to BHF-containing serum to quantify inflammatory cytokines. RESULTS: Ten key components and 169 intersecting targets were obtained, with enrichment in the NF-kappaB, IL-17, and MAPK pathways. Docking indicated favorable binding (< -7.0 kcal/mol) of core components to targets such as AKT1 and TNF. In model rats, inflammation increased in a time-dependent manner, accompanied by elevated TLR4 and NF-kappaB in both adipose and ovarian tissues, whereas BHF-containing serum suppressed TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, IL-6, and IL-8 in GCs. This effect was more pronounced at the early stage (6 weeks) than at the later stage (12 weeks). CONCLUSION: BHF may attenuate early chronic low-grade inflammation in PCOS, likely through modulation of the TLR4/NF-kappaB axis, supporting its potential value for early intervention.

摘要翻译

目的：本研究采用网络药理学与实验相结合的策略，探讨补肾化痰方（Bushen Huatan Formula, BHF）如何缓解肥胖型多囊卵巢综合征（polycystic ovary syndrome, PCOS）的早期慢性低度炎症。方法：从TCMSP数据库检索BHF的候选成分和靶点，并进行蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用、GO和KEGG富集分析以及分子对接（molecular docking）分析。随后，在来曲唑诱导的PCOS大鼠模型中验证预测结果，于第6周和第12周检查卵巢和脂肪组织病理学及TLR4/NF-kappaB表达；进一步将分离的颗粒细胞（granulosa cells, GCs）暴露于含BHF血清中，以定量炎症细胞因子。结果：获得10个关键成分和169个交集靶点，富集于NF-kappaB、IL-17和MAPK通路。对接结果表明，核心成分与AKT1和TNF等靶点具有良好结合亲和力（< -7.0 kcal/mol）。在模型大鼠中，炎症呈时间依赖性增加，伴随脂肪和卵巢组织中TLR4和NF-kappaB表达升高，而含BHF血清抑制了GCs中TNF-alpha、IL-1beta、IL-6和IL-8的表达。该效应在早期阶段（第6周）比后期阶段（第12周）更为显著。结论：BHF可能通过调节TLR4/NF-kappaB轴减轻PCOS的早期慢性低度炎症，支持其在早期干预中的潜在价值。
