A novel Ru(II) complex with pyrene-1-carbaldehyde hydrazone: structural, electronic, theoretical, and biological studies.

Perendija, Stefan; Dimic, Dusan; Milenkovic, Dejan; Nakarada, Djura; Djikic, Dragoslava; Suboticki, Tijana; Chichiarelli, Silvia; Kaluderovic, Goran N; Markovic, Jasmina Dimitric
Frontiers in chemistry · 2026-09-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究新型Ru(II)配合物与DNA及蛋白的结合，含分子对接和MD模拟，但对象为小分子-核酸/蛋白互作而非蛋白质-DNA互作，方法可迁移性有限，仅边缘相关。

摘要

A new Ru(II) half-sandwich complex, 1, containing an eta(6)-p-cymene ligand and (E)-1-(pyren-1-ylmethylidene)hydrazine, was synthesized in good yield and characterized by FTIR, NMR, elemental analysis, and density functional theory calculations. The close agreement between experimental and theoretical FTIR, (1)H NMR, and (13)C NMR data confirmed the proposed structure, while geometry optimization revealed the expected pseudo-octahedral piano-stool arrangement around the Ru(II) center. QTAIM analysis indicated that the metal-ligand interactions, particularly Ru-N and Ru-arene bonds, possess partial covalent character and play a key role in stabilizing the complex. Biological interaction studies showed that 1 binds spontaneously to human serum albumin (HSA) with moderate affinity via predominantly hydrophobic interactions and also binds calf thymus DNA, as demonstrated by spectrofluorimetric titration and ethidium bromide displacement experiments, suggesting mixed intercalative and groove-binding behavior. Molecular docking revealed that 1 binds most strongly to BCL-2/BCL-xL among all investigated targets, while also showing good affinity toward JAK2, KRAS, and BRAF. The docking protocol was supported by the close agreement between calculated and experimental binding free energies for HSA and DNA. Molecular dynamics and MM/PBSA analyses further confirmed that the 1-BCL-2/BCL-xL complex is stable and thermodynamically favorable, indicating that this anti-apoptotic protein may represent the most relevant biological target of complex 1. EPR measurements revealed selective radical-scavenging activity, with pronounced efficiency toward hydroxyl radicals but much weaker effects against DPPH, superoxide, and ascorbyl radicals. In vitro studies further demonstrated cytotoxic activity of the complex in several cancer cell lines, with the strongest effect observed in MIA PaCa-2 cells, accompanied by increased lipid peroxidation, protein oxidation, and alterations in matrix metalloproteinase activity, indicating that oxidative stress may contribute to its biological effects. The results identify 1 as a structurally well-defined Ru(II) compound with promising protein- and DNA-binding properties, selective antioxidant behavior, and moderate anticancer potential.

摘要翻译

一种新型Ru(II)半夹心配合物1，含有eta(6)-对伞花烃配体和(E)-1-(芘-1-基亚甲基)肼，以良好产率合成，并通过FTIR、NMR、元素分析和密度泛函理论计算进行表征。实验与理论FTIR、¹H NMR和¹³C NMR数据的紧密吻合证实了所提出的结构，而几何优化揭示了Ru(II)中心周围预期的伪八面体钢琴凳排列。QTAIM分析表明，金属-配体相互作用，特别是Ru-N和Ru-芳烃键，具有部分共价特征，并在稳定配合物中发挥关键作用。生物相互作用研究表明，1通过主要疏水相互作用以中等亲和力自发结合人血清白蛋白（HSA），并能结合小牛胸腺DNA，如荧光分光光度滴定和溴化乙锭置换实验所示，提示混合嵌入和沟槽结合行为。分子对接揭示，在所有研究靶点中，1与BCL-2/BCL-xL结合最强，同时对JAK2、KRAS和BRAF也表现出良好亲和力。对接方案得到HSA和DNA计算与实验结合自由能之间紧密一致性的支持。分子动力学和MM/PBSA分析进一步证实，1-BCL-2/BCL-xL复合物稳定且热力学有利，表明这种抗凋亡蛋白可能是配合物1最相关的生物靶点。EPR测量显示选择性自由基清除活性，对羟基自由基具有显著效率，但对DPPH、超氧和抗坏血酸自由基的作用较弱。体外研究进一步证明该配合物在多种癌细胞系中具有细胞毒性活性，其中在MIA PaCa-2细胞中观察到最强效应，伴随脂质过氧化增加、蛋白质氧化以及基质金属蛋白酶活性改变，表明氧化应激可能有助于其生物效应。结果将1确定为结构明确的Ru(II)化合物，具有有前景的蛋白质和DNA结合特性、选择性抗氧化行为以及中等抗癌潜力。
