BpCYCD3;1 regulates organ growth by activating the cell cycle module in white birch.

Yue, Linan; Wang, Xinyu; Zheng, Zhimin
Plant science : an international journal of experimental plant biology · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究白桦中BpCYCD3;1通过激活CDKA-RBR-E2F模块调控器官生长，涉及E2F转录因子与下游启动子结合，但核心是细胞周期调控而非TF-DNA互作机制建模，且方法以实验为主，仅含简单对接分析，与课题聚焦的AI/物理模拟方法不匹配。

摘要

Cyclin D proteins are key regulators of cell cycle progression and plant growth. Here, we identified BpCYCD3;1, a CYCD3 family member in birch, and investigated its function. Phylogenetic and sequence analyses showed that BpCYCD3;1 is evolutionarily conserved and contains canonical cell cycle-related domains. Promoter and expression analyses suggested responsiveness to environmental and hormonal signals, with preferential expression in leaves. BpCYCD3;1 overexpression promoted leaf expansion and biomass accumulation, whereas CRISPR/Cas9 knockout reduced vegetative growth. Cytological analyses revealed increased cell density and epidermal cell number in overexpression lines, indicating enhanced cell proliferation. At the molecular level, BpCYCD3;1 upregulated G1/S transition regulators and DNA replication-related genes. Yeast two-hybrid and docking analyses suggested interaction between BpCYCD3;1 and CDKA;1, as well as the interaction between RBR and E2F transcription factors, and binding of E2FA/E2FB to downstream promoters. Together, these results indicate that BpCYCD3;1 promotes vegetative growth by activating the CDKA-RBR-E2F regulatory module, providing a potential target for improving tree growth and biomass production.

摘要翻译

Cyclin D蛋白是细胞周期进程和植物生长的关键调控因子。本研究鉴定了桦树中CYCD3家族成员BpCYCD3;1，并探究了其功能。系统发育和序列分析表明，BpCYCD3;1在进化上保守，并包含典型的细胞周期相关结构域。启动子和表达分析提示其对环境和激素信号具有响应性，且在叶片中优先表达。BpCYCD3;1过表达促进了叶片扩展和生物量积累，而CRISPR/Cas9敲除则减少了营养生长。细胞学分析显示，过表达株系中细胞密度和表皮细胞数量增加，表明细胞增殖增强。在分子水平上，BpCYCD3;1上调了G1/S期转换调控因子和DNA复制相关基因。酵母双杂交和对接分析提示BpCYCD3;1与CDKA;1之间存在相互作用，以及RBR与E2F转录因子之间的相互作用，并且E2FA/E2FB与下游启动子结合。综上，这些结果表明BpCYCD3;1通过激活CDKA-RBR-E2F调控模块促进营养生长，为改善树木生长和生物量生产提供了潜在靶点。
