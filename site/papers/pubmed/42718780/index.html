Secoisolariciresinol diglucoside alleviates inflammatory injury in osteoarthritis chondrocytes through ERBB2-associated JAK2/STAT3 signaling: network pharmacology and experimental validation.

Hu, Haibei; Li, Gaoshan; Wang, Yangxu; Xie, Suyu; Cao, Feng; Li, Huanyu; Gao, Xubin
Frontiers in immunology · 2026-09-10 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究植物化合物SDG通过ERBB2/JAK2/STAT3信号通路缓解骨关节炎软骨细胞炎症损伤，涉及分子对接但核心是细胞实验与信号通路验证，非蛋白质-DNA互作或TF-DNA结合机制，且无AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Cartilage injury is a hallmark of osteoarthritis (OA). erb-B2 receptor tyrosine kinase 2 (ERBB2) has been reported to be involved in mediating the therapeutic effects of polyphenols in orthopedic diseases. This study aimed to investigate the protective effects and underlying mechanisms of action of ERBB2 in OA chondrocytes. METHODS: Bioinformatics analysis and molecular docking were used to predict the binding relationship between ERBB2 and the polyphenolic compound, secoisolariciresinol diglucoside (SDG). ATDC5 cells were induced with insulin-transferrin-selenium (ITS), followed by lipopolysaccharide (LPS) exposure to mimic OA inflammation, and treated with SDG. Cell viability, collagen II expression, apoptosis, interleukin 6 (IL-6)/Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha (TNF-alpha) secretion, and malondialdehyde (MDA)/superoxide dismutase (SOD) levels were evaluated using cell counting kit-8 (CCK-8), immunofluorescence, flow cytometry, enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA), and commercial kits. Matrix metalloproteinase 13 (MMP13) and ERBB2 mRNA levels were assessed by quantitative real-time polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR). Extracellular matrix integrity was assessed by toluidine blue staining. The predicted SDG-ERBB2 interaction was validated using a cellular thermal shift assay (CETSA). Western blotting was performed to assess the expression of ERBB2 and JAK2/STAT3. Rescue experiments with ERBB2 overexpression or colivelin (a JAK/STAT activator) treatment confirmed the involvement of this pathway. RESULTS: SDG dose-dependently reversed the LPS-induced reduction in cell viability and collagen II expression, while suppressing apoptosis, IL-6/TNF-alpha secretion, and MDA levels, and increasing SOD activity. SDG significantly reduced MMP13 mRNA levels and enhanced extracellular matrix proteoglycan deposition, indicating the preservation of matrix integrity. ERBB2 has been identified as a candidate target for SDG. SDG inhibits ERBB2 protein expression and reduces JAK2 and STAT3 phosphorylation ERBB2 overexpression and colivelin treatment abrogated the protective effects of SDG on cell viability, collagen II expression, apoptosis, inflammation, and oxidative stress, as well as its suppression of MMP13 and preservation of matrix integrity. CONCLUSION: SDG protects chondrocytes against LPS-induced inflammatory injury by targeting ERBB2 and suppressing the downstream JAK2/STAT3 signaling pathway. These findings provide mechanistic evidence supporting the chondroprotective potential of SDG under inflammatory conditions and suggest that ERBB2/JAK2/STAT3 signaling may represent a potential therapeutic target in OA.

摘要翻译

背景：软骨损伤是骨关节炎（osteoarthritis, OA）的标志性特征。erb-B2受体酪氨酸激酶2（ERBB2）已被报道参与介导多酚类化合物在骨科疾病中的治疗效果。本研究旨在探讨ERBB2在OA软骨细胞中的保护作用及其潜在作用机制。方法：采用生物信息学分析和分子对接（molecular docking）预测ERBB2与多酚类化合物开环异落叶松树脂酚二葡萄糖苷（secoisolariciresinol diglucoside, SDG）之间的结合关系。用胰岛素-转铁蛋白-硒（insulin-transferrin-selenium, ITS）诱导ATDC5细胞，随后用脂多糖（lipopolysaccharide, LPS）暴露以模拟OA炎症，并用SDG处理。使用细胞计数试剂盒-8（CCK-8）、免疫荧光、流式细胞术、酶联免疫吸附试验（ELISA）和商业试剂盒评估细胞活力、II型胶原表达、凋亡、白细胞介素6（IL-6）/肿瘤坏死因子-α（TNF-alpha）分泌以及丙二醛（MDA）/超氧化物歧化酶（SOD）水平。通过定量实时聚合酶链反应（qRT-PCR）评估基质金属蛋白酶13（MMP13）和ERBB2 mRNA水平。通过甲苯胺蓝染色评估细胞外基质完整性。使用细胞热转变分析（cellular thermal shift assay, CETSA）验证预测的SDG-ERBB2相互作用。通过蛋白质印迹法评估ERBB2及JAK2/STAT3的表达。通过ERBB2过表达或colivelin（一种JAK/STAT激活剂）处理的拯救实验确认该通路的参与。结果：SDG剂量依赖性地逆转了LPS诱导的细胞活力和II型胶原表达降低，同时抑制凋亡、IL-6/TNF-alpha分泌和MDA水平，并增加SOD活性。SDG显著降低MMP13 mRNA水平并增强细胞外基质蛋白聚糖沉积，表明基质完整性得以保持。ERBB2被确定为SDG的候选靶点。SDG抑制ERBB2蛋白表达并减少JAK2和STAT3磷酸化。ERBB2过表达和colivelin处理消除了SDG对细胞活力、II型胶原表达、凋亡、炎症和氧化应激的保护作用，以及其对MMP13的抑制和基质完整性的维持。结论：SDG通过靶向ERBB2并抑制下游JAK2/STAT3信号通路，保护软骨细胞免受LPS诱导的炎症损伤。这些发现为SDG在炎症条件下的软骨保护潜力提供了机制性证据，并提示ERBB2/JAK2/STAT3信号可能代表OA中的一个潜在治疗靶点。
