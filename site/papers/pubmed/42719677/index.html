Matrine Alleviates Sepsis-Induced Acute Lung Injury by Reinforcing NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4-Associated Anti-Ferroptotic Defenses and Attenuating NF-kappaB-Driven Inflammation.

Chen, Xue; Yuan, Min; Huang, Ting; Wang, Yuxuan; Lei, Zihan; Qing, Jingxue; Gong, Hailong; Aidebaike, Delida; Zhang, Zhengyu; Wu, Xiaojing
Drug design, development and therapy · 2026-09-10 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究苦参碱通过NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4抗铁死亡和NF-κB抗炎通路缓解脓毒症肺损伤，虽涉及分子对接和MD模拟，但核心是药物机制而非TF-DNA互作，对象和方法与本课题关联较弱。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Sepsis triggers dysregulated systemic inflammation and multiple-organ dysfunction, with the lungs being particularly susceptible to injury. Sepsis-induced acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) is mainly driven by TLR4/NF-kappaB-mediated hyperinflammation and alveolar macrophage activation. Matrine, a bioactive alkaloid derived from Sophora flavescens, has been reported to modulate redox homeostasis and ferroptosis-associated lipid peroxidation. However, the target-specific mechanisms underlying its effects on ferroptosis and inflammatory signaling in sepsis-induced acute lung injury (SALI) remain incompletely understood. PURPOSE: This study aimed to evaluate the therapeutic effects of matrine in a cecal ligation and puncture (CLP)-induced SALI model and to determine whether its protective effects involve reinforcement of NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4-associated anti-ferroptotic defenses and suppression of NF-kappaB-driven inflammation. METHODS: We analyzed the single-cell RNA-sequencing (scRNA-seq) dataset GSE273924 to characterize CD45-enriched pulmonary immune-cell subsets in sham mice and mice with intratracheal Escherichia coli-induced pneumonia. Network pharmacology and Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG) enrichment analyses were performed to predict Kushen (KS)-related targets and pathways associated with SALI. Differential expression analysis and weighted gene co-expression network analysis (WGCNA) of GSE245013 were used to identify candidate targets. Matrine-NQO1 binding and intracellular target engagement were evaluated using molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, surface plasmon resonance (SPR), and the cellular thermal shift assay (CETSA). The therapeutic effects of matrine were assessed in mice with CLP-induced SALI and in lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-stimulated MH-S cells. Lung histopathology, inflammatory cytokine production, target protein expression, ferroptosis-associated indicators, and NF-kappaB activation were evaluated using molecular, biochemical, and histological assays. The functional contribution of NQO1 was further examined using the NQO1 inhibitor ES936. RESULTS: scRNA-seq analysis of GSE273924 revealed substantial remodeling of the CD45-enriched pulmonary immune-cell landscape in mice with intratracheal E. coli-induced pneumonia, including macrophage transcriptional programs associated with ferroptosis and inflammatory signaling. Integrated network pharmacology and bioinformatics analyses prioritized NQO1 as a candidate target of matrine and identified NF-kappaB signaling as a potentially relevant pathway. Molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations, SPR, and CETSA supported matrine-NQO1 binding and intracellular target engagement. Functionally, matrine improved survival, attenuated lung injury, reinforced NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4-associated anti-ferroptotic defenses, and suppressed NF-kappaB activation in CLP mice. Similar protective effects were observed in LPS-stimulated MH-S cells. ES936 partially attenuated the matrine-mediated improvements in cell viability, redox homeostasis, ferroptosis-associated indicators, and NF-kappaB p65 phosphorylation, supporting a functional contribution of NQO1 to the protective effects of matrine. CONCLUSION: Matrine alleviates SALI by reinforcing NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4-associated anti-ferroptotic defenses and attenuating NF-kappaB-driven inflammation.

摘要翻译

背景：脓毒症引发失调的全身性炎症和多器官功能障碍，其中肺尤其易受损伤。脓毒症诱导的急性呼吸窘迫综合征（ARDS）主要由TLR4/NF-kappaB介导的过度炎症和肺泡巨噬细胞活化驱动。苦参碱（matrine）是从苦参（Sophora flavescens）中提取的一种生物活性生物碱，已被报道可调节氧化还原稳态和铁死亡相关的脂质过氧化。然而，其在脓毒症诱导的急性肺损伤（SALI）中对铁死亡和炎症信号传导的靶点特异性机制仍未完全阐明。目的：本研究旨在评估苦参碱在盲肠结扎穿刺（CLP）诱导的SALI模型中的治疗效果，并确定其保护作用是否涉及增强NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4相关的抗铁死亡防御和抑制NF-kappaB驱动的炎症。方法：我们分析了单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq）数据集GSE273924，以表征假手术小鼠和气管内大肠杆菌诱导肺炎小鼠中CD45富集的肺免疫细胞亚群。进行了网络药理学和京都基因与基因组百科全书（KEGG）富集分析，以预测与SALI相关的苦参（KS）靶点和通路。利用GSE245013的差异表达分析和加权基因共表达网络分析（WGCNA）来识别候选靶点。通过分子对接、分子动力学模拟、表面等离子体共振（SPR）和细胞热位移分析（CETSA）评估了苦参碱-NQO1结合及细胞内靶点参与。在CLP诱导的SALI小鼠和脂多糖（LPS）刺激的MH-S细胞中评估了苦参碱的治疗效果。使用分子、生化和组织学检测评估了肺组织病理学、炎症细胞因子产生、靶蛋白表达、铁死亡相关指标和NF-kappaB活化。进一步使用NQO1抑制剂ES936检查了NQO1的功能贡献。结果：GSE273924的scRNA-seq分析显示，在气管内大肠杆菌诱导肺炎的小鼠中，CD45富集的肺免疫细胞景观发生了显著重塑，包括与铁死亡和炎症信号传导相关的巨噬细胞转录程序。综合网络药理学和生物信息学分析将NQO1优先列为苦参碱的候选靶点，并确定NF-kappaB信号传导为潜在相关通路。分子对接、分子动力学模拟、SPR和CETSA支持苦参碱-NQO1结合及细胞内靶点参与。功能上，苦参碱改善了CLP小鼠的存活率，减轻了肺损伤，增强了NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4相关的抗铁死亡防御，并抑制了NF-kappaB活化。在LPS刺激的MH-S细胞中观察到类似的保护效果。ES936部分减弱了苦参碱介导的细胞活力、氧化还原稳态、铁死亡相关指标和NF-kappaB p65磷酸化的改善，支持NQO1对苦参碱保护作用的功能贡献。结论：苦参碱通过增强NQO1/SLC7A11/GPX4相关的抗铁死亡防御和减弱NF-kappaB驱动的炎症来缓解SALI。
