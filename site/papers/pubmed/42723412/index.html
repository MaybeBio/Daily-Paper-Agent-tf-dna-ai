An Integrated Analysis of Manganese Metabolism-Related Genes and Their Association With Biomarkers, Immune Infiltration, and Clinical Subtypes in Alzheimer's Disease.

Shu, Shengnan; Hu, Jiahao; Hu, Shanshan; Yao, Yefeng
Genes, brain, and behavior · 2026-09-11 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究阿尔茨海默病中锰代谢相关基因，虽涉及转录因子调控网络构建，但核心是疾病诊断标志物与免疫浸润分析，非蛋白质-DNA互作或TF-DNA结合机制研究，且方法以传统生物信息学与机器学习为主，无AI/物理模拟建模。

摘要

Alzheimer's disease (AD), a neurodegenerative condition marked by amyloid-beta plaques and tau protein neurofibrillary tangles, progresses against a backdrop of essential biological processes. Manganese, an indispensable trace element for vital functions including energy metabolism and antioxidant defense, is integral to neurological health. Its precise metabolic role throughout the course of AD pathogenesis is not fully elucidated. Four essential manganese metabolism-related genes were identified as diagnostic markers through a multi-omic framework. This approach integrated Weighted Gene Co-Expression Network Analysis with machine learning ensembles (Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator/Random Forest/Extreme Gradient Boosting), followed by rigorous testing in an independent dataset. Beyond identification, we utilized CIBERSORT and ssGSEA to characterize immune infiltration and leveraged GSEA/GO/KEGG for pathway elucidation. Finally, AD patients were stratified into molecular subgroups based on these hub genes, and their underlying regulatory networks involving miRNAs and transcription factors were reconstructed. An integrated bioinformatics framework identified 12 differentially expressed genes related to manganese metabolism in AD. Machine-learning-based feature selection further pinpointed four key diagnostic biomarkers (TSPO, PTBP1, GLO1, and ACACB) with high discriminative power (AUC: 0.831-0.905). Immune infiltration analysis revealed substantial immune remodeling in AD, including an elevation of naive B cells. Moreover, AD samples were classified into two molecular subtypes displaying distinct immune and metabolic characteristics, and regulatory miRNA/TF interaction networks were constructed for the core genes. Our results shed new light on the molecular mechanisms underlying AD and support future efforts toward early diagnosis and personalized therapeutic interventions based on molecular subtypes.

摘要翻译

阿尔茨海默病（Alzheimer's disease, AD）是一种以β-淀粉样蛋白斑块和tau蛋白神经原纤维缠结为特征的神经退行性疾病，其进展伴随着一系列关键生物学过程的变化。锰（manganese）是维持能量代谢和抗氧化防御等生命功能不可或缺的微量元素，对神经系统健康至关重要。其在AD发病机制全过程中的精确代谢作用尚未完全阐明。通过多组学框架，鉴定出四个与锰代谢相关的关键基因作为诊断标志物。该方法整合了加权基因共表达网络分析（Weighted Gene Co-Expression Network Analysis）与机器学习集成方法（最小绝对收缩和选择算子/随机森林/极端梯度提升，Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator/Random Forest/Extreme Gradient Boosting），并在独立数据集中进行了严格验证。除基因鉴定外，我们利用CIBERSORT和ssGSEA表征免疫浸润情况，并借助GSEA/GO/KEGG进行通路解析。最后，基于这些核心基因将AD患者分为分子亚型，并重建了涉及miRNA和转录因子的潜在调控网络。一个整合的生物信息学框架在AD中鉴定出12个与锰代谢相关的差异表达基因。基于机器学习的特征选择进一步精确定位了四个关键诊断生物标志物（TSPO、PTBP1、GLO1和ACACB），其具有高区分能力（AUC: 0.831-0.905）。免疫浸润分析揭示了AD中显著的免疫重塑，包括初始B细胞（naive B cells）的升高。此外，AD样本被分为两个分子亚型，显示出不同的免疫和代谢特征，并为核心基因构建了调控性miRNA/TF相互作用网络。我们的结果为AD的分子机制提供了新见解，并支持未来基于分子亚型进行早期诊断和个性化治疗干预的努力。
