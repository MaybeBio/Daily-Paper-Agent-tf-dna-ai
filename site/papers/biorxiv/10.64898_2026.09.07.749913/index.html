TransBind2: Improving Transcription Factor-DNA Binding Prediction with Multimodal Data and Bidirectional Cross Attention

Shreya Basnet; Jianlin Cheng
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-14 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出TransBind2，融合TF序列/结构、DNA序列与染色质可及性等多模态数据，通过双向交叉注意力改进TF-DNA结合预测，高度契合本课题的TF-DNA互作建模与AI方法核心。

摘要

Accurate genome-wide prediction of transcription factor (TF)–DNA binding remains challenging because many models focus mainly on DNA sequence and overlook chromatin context and TF structure. We previously developed TransBind, a protein-aware model that combines TF and DNA representations through cross-attention. Here, we introduce TransBind2, which improves on TransBind in several ways. It incorporates DNase-seq accessibility and genome mappability tracks as additional input, uses a biomodal protein language model (ProstT5) to capture both TF sequence and structure, and applies bidirectional cross-attention so DNA and protein features can refine each other. We also frame prediction as binary classification of individual < DNA bin, TF, cell type > triplets, allowing the model to generalize to new TFs and cell types. Across 690 human ChIP-seq experiments covering 161 TFs and 91 cell types, TransBind2 achieves a macro AUROC of 0.9648 and AUPR of 0.4215, outperforming TransBind and other baselines, with a ≥12.67% relative AUPR gain. The model trained on human data also performs well in cross-species zero-shot prediction on mouse data. Saliency analysis shows that it can identify TF-binding peaks with a median error of 12–38 base pairs (bps) despite being trained on window-level labels. Ablation studies further show that TF structure, chromatin accessibility, and bidirectional attention each improve performance. Overall, these results show that combining TF structure with chromatin context leads to more accurate and generalizable TF–DNA binding predictions.

摘要翻译

准确的基因组范围内转录因子（TF）–DNA结合预测仍具挑战性，因为许多模型主要关注DNA序列，而忽视了染色质环境和TF结构。我们先前开发了TransBind，一种通过交叉注意力结合TF和DNA表征的蛋白质感知模型。在此，我们引入TransBind2，它在多个方面对TransBind进行了改进。它纳入DNase-seq可及性和基因组可映射性轨迹作为额外输入，使用双峰蛋白质语言模型（ProstT5）来捕获TF序列和结构，并应用双向交叉注意力，使DNA和蛋白质特征能够相互优化。我们还将预测框架化为对单个<DNA bin, TF, 细胞类型>三元组的二元分类，使模型能够泛化到新的TF和细胞类型。在覆盖161种TF和91种细胞类型的690个人类ChIP-seq实验中，TransBind2实现了宏AUROC为0.9648和AUPR为0.4215，优于TransBind和其他基线，相对AUPR增益≥12.67%。在人类数据上训练的模型在小鼠数据的跨物种零样本预测中也表现良好。显著性分析显示，尽管仅在窗口级标签上训练，它仍能以12–38碱基对（bps）的中位误差识别TF结合峰。消融研究进一步表明，TF结构、染色质可及性和双向注意力各自提升了性能。总体而言，这些结果表明，将TF结构与染色质环境相结合可带来更准确且更具泛化性的TF–DNA结合预测。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何准确且可泛化地预测基因组范围内TF–DNA结合位点，尤其是对未见过的TF和细胞类型？
  • 为什么重要：TF–DNA结合是基因调控的核心事件，准确预测对理解疾病机制、非编码变异功能注释和调控网络重建至关重要。现有模型多依赖DNA序列，忽略染色质上下文和TF结构信息，导致泛化能力有限。
  • 现有方法不足：①多数模型仅用DNA序列（如DeepSEA、Enformer），缺乏蛋白信息；②蛋白感知模型（如TransBind）虽引入TF表示，但未利用TF结构，且交叉注意力为单向；③缺乏对染色质可及性和mappability的系统整合；④难以泛化到新TF/细胞类型。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a multimodal model that integrates TF structure, chromatin accessibility, and bidirectional cross-attention achieve higher accuracy and better generalization for TF–DNA binding prediction across TFs, cell types, and species than sequence-only or unidirectional-attention baselines?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：构建多模态深度学习模型，输入DNA序列（one-hot）、DNase-seq信号、mappability track、TF蛋白序列（经ProstT5编码），通过双向交叉注意力融合DNA和蛋白特征，输出三元组的二分类概率。
  2. 核心洞察：TF–DNA结合是「序列基序 + 染色质可及性 + TF结构偏好」三者共同决定的。将TF结构信息（而非仅序列）和染色质上下文显式编码，并允许DNA与蛋白特征双向交互，能更完整地建模结合机制。将预测任务重构为三元组分类，使模型学习TF和细胞类型的独立表示，从而支持组合泛化。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：①多模态融合中，「模态间交互方向」比「模态数量」更重要——双向注意力优于单向；②蛋白语言模型（如ProstT5）可同时编码序列和结构，是连接序列与结构信息的有效桥梁；③将预测任务分解为「实体组合」（TF×细胞类型×DNA bin）而非「整体分类」，可显著提升组合泛化能力。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：预测三元组的结合概率，非碱基级结合位点预测（saliency可近似定位）。
  • oracle/真值输入：ChIP-seq实验标签（结合/非结合），DNase-seq和mappability为真实实验数据，非预测值。
  • 端到端状态：端到端训练，但依赖外部工具ProstT5（预训练蛋白语言模型）作为固定编码器。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖690个人类ChIP-seq实验，覆盖161 TF/91细胞类型，但未说明数据划分策略（如TF/细胞类型是否在训练/测试中分离）。
  • 模型依赖：依赖ProstT5的表示质量；若ProstT5对某些TF结构编码不佳，可能影响预测。
  • 最难情形：跨物种零样本（人类→小鼠）是强泛化测试，但未提供具体性能数值。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅覆盖人类和小鼠；未涉及其他物种、非ChIP-seq实验类型（如CUT&Tag）、TF家族覆盖度未说明。
  • 不确定性：未提供置信区间、方差或统计检验。
  • 有边界的复述：在690个人类ChIP-seq实验上，TransBind2通过融合TF结构（ProstT5）、DNase-seq可及性和mappability，并采用双向交叉注意力，达到宏AUROC 0.9648、AUPR 0.4215，较前作TransBind有≥12.67%相对AUPR提升；该模型可零样本迁移至小鼠数据，且saliency分析可定位结合峰至12–38 bp中位误差。这些结论限于所覆盖的TF、细胞类型和物种，且未提供统计显著性证据。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a plausible and potentially valuable extension of a prior model, with a sensible design rationale and strong reported performance. However, the evidence base available for review is limited to the abstract, which prevents independent verification of the core claims. Several claims, particularly the AUPR gain, cross-species generalization, and saliency-based localization, require detailed methodological and statistical support that is not visible here. The work is likely of interest to the computational genomics and gene regulation communities, but the case is not fully established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and bioinformaticians working on TF–DNA binding prediction, regulatory genomics, and deep learning applications to genomics. Researchers developing protein-aware or multimodal models for sequence-function prediction would also find the architectural choices relevant. The cross-species generalization claim may interest those studying model transferability across organisms.
  • Major strengths The design rationale is clear and well motivated, addressing a recognized limitation in the field by incorporating chromatin context and TF structure. The use of a bidirectional cross-attention mechanism and a protein language model is a reasonable and current approach. The evaluation scale, 690 ChIP-seq experiments across 161 TFs and 91 cell types, is substantial. The inclusion of ablation studies and cross-species zero-shot testing indicates attention to model interpretability and generalizability.
