Accurate modeling of ion-protein binding in solution: molecular mechanics force field innovation and validation on Mg2+

Giorgio Schillaci; Julian Mélendez Delgado; Matthew Saunders; Irene Arias Gutierrez; Péter R. Nagy; Sameer Varma
ChemRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-18 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文聚焦离子-蛋白结合力场建模，虽涉及蛋白结合机制但非蛋白质-DNA互作体系，且方法为分子力学力场创新，与TF-DNA互作课题关联较弱。

摘要

Multivalent ions bind to proteins to control many biological processes. Molecular simulations can effectively supplement experiments to resolve underlying mechanisms, but suffer from reliability and feasibility issues in describing ion-protein binding in the condensed phase. Here we present a molecular mechanics based generalizable solution to this long-standing challenge. Using the divalent Mg 2+ as a model, we demonstrate that our approach yields unprecedented accuracy in describing its binding to proteins in solution – compared to high level quantum mechanics, the mean error in local interaction energies is 0.5%, and the binding modes and binding free energies match experiment. Our study also shows that key to achieving this accuracy requires a delicate balance between the large repulsive and attractive forces at short distances near ions for which explicit modeling of polarization is essential. Overall, our work lays foundation for future development of molecular simulation methods for ions and opens the way to quantitatively study Mg-dependent mechanisms in hundreds of enzymes and proteins.

摘要翻译

多价离子与蛋白质结合以调控众多生物过程。分子模拟能有效补充实验以解析潜在机制，但在凝聚相中描述离子-蛋白质结合时面临可靠性和可行性问题。在此，我们提出一种基于分子力学的通用解决方案，以应对这一长期挑战。以二价Mg²⁺为模型，我们证明该方法在描述其与溶液中蛋白质结合时达到了前所未有的精度——与高水平量子力学相比，局部相互作用能的平均误差为0.5%，且结合模式和结合自由能与实验吻合。我们的研究还表明，实现这一精度的关键在于离子附近短距离内大排斥力和吸引力之间的微妙平衡，而显式建模极化对此至关重要。总体而言，我们的工作为未来离子分子模拟方法的发展奠定了基础，并为定量研究数百种酶和蛋白质中Mg依赖性机制开辟了道路。
